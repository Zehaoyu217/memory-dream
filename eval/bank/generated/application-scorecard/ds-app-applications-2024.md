# ds-app-applications-2024: 214,600 new-account applications, Jan to Dec 2024, with 12-month outcome
Applications from the decision log, outcome from `cards.accounts_monthly` for booked accounts (90+ DPD in 12 months).
Booked accounts 58,300; declined applications carry no observed outcome and are handled by reject inference.
Bad rate among booked accounts 4.6%; the base for every application model in this project.
