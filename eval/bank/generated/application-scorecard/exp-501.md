# exp-501: parcelling 8,400 manual approvals lifts KS from 0.36 to 0.38 on holdout-2025q1 (n=21,300)
Reject inference by parcelling the reject-sample outcomes back into the rejected population.
Eval: holdout-2025q1, n=21,300, KS 0.36 for the baseline and 0.38 with parcelling.
Adopted as the weight scheme for app-gbm-v2.
