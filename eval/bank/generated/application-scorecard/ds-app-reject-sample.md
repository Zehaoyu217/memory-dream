# ds-app-reject-sample: 8,400 manually approved rejects from credit committee, 2024 to early 2025
Rejected applications that the credit committee approved by hand, so their outcome is observed.
Sample is not random: it is weighted to scores just under the 585 cutoff.
Used to estimate reject behaviour in exp-501; bad rate among these 8,400 is 7.9%.
