# exp-107: fuzzy matching covers more long-tail merchants, exact rules are more precise
Long-tail slice of holdout-2025q2 (under 20 transactions in 90 days, n=5,200 merchants).
Fuzzy descriptor match: 79% coverage at 0.88 precision. Exact MCC-rule lookup: 41% coverage at 0.97 precision.
Combined rule (exact first, fuzzy fallback above 0.85 similarity) gave 84% coverage at 0.92 precision in a follow-up run.
Source: `cards.merchants` joined to the labelled slice.
