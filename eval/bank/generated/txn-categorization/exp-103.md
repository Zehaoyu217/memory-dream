# exp-103: LightGBM with the same inputs gives macro-F1 0.78
LightGBM (600 trees, depth 8) on feat-txc-descriptor-tokens-v1 plus feat-txc-mcc-prior, holdout-2025q2 (n=2,000).
Macro-F1 0.78 (+0.04 over the logistic model), accuracy 0.85.
Training took 14 minutes on a CPU node; no GPU needed.
