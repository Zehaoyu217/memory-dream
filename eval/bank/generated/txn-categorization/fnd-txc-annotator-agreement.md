# Annotator agreement is kappa 0.82; most disagreement is Food delivery vs Restaurants
Cohen's kappa 0.82 across the three annotators on 1,200 merchants labelled twice.
61% of disagreements are between 'Food delivery' and 'Restaurants'; both labels are kept as separate classes.
Agreement on Fuel and Travel is above 0.9.
This sets the realistic ceiling: about 0.85 macro-F1 on holdout-2025q2.
