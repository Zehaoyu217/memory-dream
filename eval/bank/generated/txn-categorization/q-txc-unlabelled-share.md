# How much spend does txn-categorization leave in 'other', and what is the target?
Open as of Dec 2025. With txc-gbm-v2 in place, 9.4% of Q4-2025 monthly spend lands in the 'other' bucket.
The working target is under 5% of monthly spend. Most of the residue is long-tail merchants (fnd-txc-long-tail).
Measured on `cards.txn_daily` joined to the category output, spend-weighted.
