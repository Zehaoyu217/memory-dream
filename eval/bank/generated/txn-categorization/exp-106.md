# exp-106: merchant-history features score 0.91 on a random split but 0.78 on a time split
txc-gbm-v2 plus feat-txc-merchant-history-v1: macro-F1 0.91 on a random 80/20 split of the labelled set.
On a time split (train to 2025-06, test on 2025-Q3 merchants, n=2,400) the same model scores 0.78, against 0.77 without history features.
The random split leaks: the same merchant appears in train and test with the same history.
Led to fnd-txc-history-leaks.
