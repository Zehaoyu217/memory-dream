# 18,400 merchants from cards.merchants labelled into 23 categories
Three annotators labelled every merchant in the `cards.merchants` extract (snapshot 2025-01-31) into one of 23 spend categories.
Labels live in the annotation sheet `txc_labels_2025q1`; a merchant is kept only when two of three annotators agree (16,950 kept).
Merchant names were masked to descriptor tokens before labelling; no cardholder IDs are attached.
Source for every training set and for holdout-2025q2 (ds-txc-gold-holdout).
