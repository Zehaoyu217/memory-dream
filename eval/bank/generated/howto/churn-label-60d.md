# Churn meant no purchase or payment for 60 days after observation (superseded)
Churn in `cards.churn_labels` was defined as no purchase and no payment in the 60 days after the observation date. The 2025 retention models were trained on this label and the monthly churn line used it too.
Superseded by `howto/churn-label-90d`. Dormancy over the holiday season inflated this label.
