# KS and Gini go in the monthly review, with KS as the headline
Report KS and Gini side by side for each model, where Gini = 2 x AUC - 1. KS is the headline because it is read at the operating point, while AUC and Gini only summarise ranking. Both are computed on the OOT set, with n in the footnote.
