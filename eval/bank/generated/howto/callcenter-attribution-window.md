# A call counts as a retention touch only if it starts within 14 days of the offer send date
Calls in `callcenter.calls` are linked to a retention offer when the call starts within 14 days after the offer send date. Calls after 14 days are attributed to other causes. Yuki Tanaka's team excludes calls that are complaints about fees from the attribution count.
