# A model card lists target, label window, eval sets, PSI status, known gaps and owner
Required fields: target and label window; training and OOT sets with their names; metrics with n; PSI status; known gaps; and the owner. The card is generated as markdown by the registration step, and Aisha Khan checks it.
Cards without a known-gaps section are rejected. The 2025 retention card omitted the 2024 holiday gap, which is why the section is now required.
