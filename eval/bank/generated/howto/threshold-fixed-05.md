# A decision cutoff of 0.5 on the score was the default until December 2025 (superseded)
The scoring service flagged accounts above a fixed 0.5 probability for review, on every model. The cutoff was never checked against review capacity or loss costs, and the collections queue overflowed in 2025 Q3.
Superseded by `howto/threshold-selection-cost-matrix`.
