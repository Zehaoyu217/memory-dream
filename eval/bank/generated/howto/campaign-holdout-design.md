# Campaign holdouts are 10% of the eligible list, drawn at random before send
Hold out 10% of the eligible population for each campaign, drawn at random before any send. Store the control rows in `marketing.campaign_responses` with `arm = control`. Chloe Martin's team keeps the holdout for the whole campaign and never swaps it for a historical list.
For a 200,000 account campaign that gives 20,000 controls, above the 3,000-per-arm minimum.
