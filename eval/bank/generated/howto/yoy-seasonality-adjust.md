# Compare spend to the same month last year, not to the previous month
For spend and payment KPIs, compare to the same calendar month last year, and show month-on-month only in the appendix. December and January dips made month-on-month comparisons read as a 6% fall every year.
Use `cards.txn_daily` with `fiscal_month` from the team calendar.
