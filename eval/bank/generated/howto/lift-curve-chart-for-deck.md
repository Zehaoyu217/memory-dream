# Lift curves in the deck plot cumulative lift by decile, with a dashed line at 1.0
Plot cumulative lift on the y-axis against decile on the x-axis, with a dashed line at 1.0 for random. Put the decile counts under the x-axis and label the top-decile value directly. Chloe Martin's deck template uses this as the standard.
