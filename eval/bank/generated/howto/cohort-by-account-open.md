# Retention cohorts are grouped by account open month (superseded)
Retention cohorts were grouped by `open_month` from `cards.accounts_monthly`. Reactivated accounts with an old open date landed in cohorts that had no early life, so month-1 retention for the 2024 cohorts read about 4 points high.
Superseded by `howto/cohort-by-first-statement`.
