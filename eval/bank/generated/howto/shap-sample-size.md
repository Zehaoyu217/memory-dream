# SHAP summaries use a stratified sample of 5,000 OOT accounts
Compute SHAP on 5,000 OOT accounts, stratified 50/50 across score decile bands 1 to 5 and 6 to 10. The full OOT set takes about four hours on the scoring box. The 5,000-account sample reproduces the top ten feature ranking of the 2026 retention model.
