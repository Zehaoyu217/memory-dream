# Randomised push holdout: pushes add +0.2 percent sessions over 28 days (n = 214,000 accounts)
Set-up: campaign sends in Nov 2025 with a 10 percent randomised holdout (_HO campaigns) that received no push.
Treated accounts averaged +0.2 percent sessions over 28 days against the holdout. The open-rate correlation of 0.31 is therefore mostly reverse causation.
Result in fnd-ae-push-null.
