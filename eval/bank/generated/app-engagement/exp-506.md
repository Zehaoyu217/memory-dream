# Gradient boosted attrition model on the score-v2 inputs: AUC 0.81 on holdout-2025q4
Set-up: gradient boosted trees on ae_act28, depth v2 and tenure, 90-day attrition label, scored on holdout-2025q4 (n = 610,000).
AUC 0.81, up from 0.76 for the linear score alone. The extra lift is mostly from non-linear activity thresholds.
Basis for ae-attr-gbm-v1.
