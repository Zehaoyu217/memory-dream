# Chat sessions joined to `callcenter.calls` on caller_key within 48 hours of session end
Join built by cx-analytics and this project. Of 312,000 contained sessions in 2025, 22 percent had a call within 48 hours.
The join uses caller_key, which is hashed; calls with no account match (3 percent) are dropped from linkage counts.
