# Can the account-servicing chatbot contain 40 percent of chats without repeat contact rising above the agent-handled 16 percent?
Yuki Tanaka (customer experience) set the target. Containment means the session ends with no handoff and no call; the window for that call was still open when this was written (see dec-cb-metric-24h and dec-cb-metric-48h).
Repeat contact is measured at 14 days across chat and `callcenter.calls`. Agent-handled baseline is 16 percent.
Answered through exp-301, exp-304 and dec-cb-handoff-v2.
