# 3,500 chat messages labelled into 22 intents; 1,100 held out as holdout-2025q3
Labelled by Yuki Tanaka's team from July to September 2025 samples. Inter-annotator kappa 0.79.
The 1,100 held-out messages are holdout-2025q3, never used in training. The other 2,400 train the intent models.
