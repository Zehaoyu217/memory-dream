# Randomised A/B, 25 percent of account-servicing traffic: calls per 1,000 eligible sessions down 6.1 percent
Run 2026-02-09 to 2026-03-16 (five weeks), n = 142,000 sessions, treatment against control. Containment on the treatment arm was 34 percent with the 24-hour call window.
Calls per 1,000 eligible sessions fell 6.1 percent in treatment against control. That is far below what 34 percent containment would imply.
