# Containment = no handoff in the session and no call in the next 48 hours, matching the repeat-contact window
Adopted by Yuki Tanaka and cx-analytics. The 48-hour window matches the repeat-contact measure used for the 16 percent agent baseline.
Containment on the v1 rule is 29 percent under this definition (exp-306).
