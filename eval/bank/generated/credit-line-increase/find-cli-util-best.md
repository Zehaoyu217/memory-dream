# Finding (overturned): utilisation at 90% or more is the best single predictor of 90-day loss, AUC 0.64
On cli-test-2025q1 (n=60,000), utilisation alone reached AUC 0.64 for 90-day loss, the highest single feature.
This was used to justify utilisation-led targeting in early 2025 planning.
Overturned by find-cli-util-not-enough (2025-10-22): alone it is not enough once payment behaviour and spend velocity are added.
