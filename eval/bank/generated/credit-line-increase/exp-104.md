# exp-104: cutoff 0.031 on the logistic score gives +$1.8M a year net margin at 1.4% 90-day loss
Sweep of cutoffs 0.020 to 0.050 on cli-logreg-baseline over the 2025-Q1 test cohort (n=60,000).
At 0.031: 90-day loss 1.4% of exposed book, net margin +$1.8M a year on the planned 2025 wave volume.
Cutoffs above 0.038 cut loss to 1.1% but lose more than half the margin.
