# Adding fee shock v1 to the driver model: Spearman 0.52 on holdout-2025q4 but 0.44 on the 2026Q1 wave
Set-up: nps-gbm-v1 plus fee shock v1. nps-gbm-v1 alone scores 0.46 on holdout-2025q4 (n = 48,210).
With fee shock v1: 0.52 on holdout-2025q4, 0.44 on the 2026Q1 wave (n = 44,870).
The drop on the later wave points to post-send fees leaking into the feature. Result in fnd-nps-fee-top.
