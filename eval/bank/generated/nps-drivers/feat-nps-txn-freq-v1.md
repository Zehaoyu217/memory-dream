# Transaction frequency: card transactions in the last 90 days, and the change over the last 60 days
Counts from `cards.txn_daily` up to send_date. Change is the ratio of the last 60 days to the prior 30 days.
Feature names in the repo: nps_txn90, nps_txn_chg60.
