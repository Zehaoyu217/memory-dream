# Raw spend scores December-peak merchants as safe, by about 0.02 on the churn score
- Accounts in the same risk band score about 0.02 lower on December snapshots than on October snapshots when their merchant mix is peak-heavy.
- Mean churn score for Dec-peak merchant accounts is 0.058, against 0.081 in October.
- This is a calendar artefact, not a change in risk. Basis: feat-csb-spend-raw on the 2024 snapshots.
