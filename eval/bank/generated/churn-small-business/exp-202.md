# exp-202: training only on 6-month-plus accounts lifts AUC on that subset to 0.699
- Evaluated on the 7,930-account subset of csb-oot-2025q1 with 6 or more months of tenure.
- Model trained on all tenures: AUC 0.687 on that subset. Trained on 6-month-plus only: AUC 0.699.
- Under-6-month accounts are scored poorly either way (AUC 0.61). Led to dec-csb-tenure-floor.
