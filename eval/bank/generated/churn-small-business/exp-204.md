# exp-204: XGBoost on raw spend reaches 0.742 AUC on the 6-month-plus subset, but ranks December merchants low
- XGBoost depth 4, 300 rounds, features spend-raw, tenure band, deposit volatility and employee cards; trained on 6-month-plus accounts.
- Holdout subset (n=7,930): AUC 0.742, KS 0.37, recall@10% 0.27.
- On the Dec 2024 snapshot, the median score for Dec-peak merchant accounts is 0.04, against 0.07 for others.
