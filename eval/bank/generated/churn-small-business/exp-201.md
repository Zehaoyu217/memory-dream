# exp-201: 90-day labels beat 60-day labels on the SMB holdout, AUC 0.684 vs 0.671
- Same LR baseline with labels at 60 and at 90 days, scored on csb-oot-2025q1 (all tenures).
- 60-day: AUC 0.671, churn 4.0%. 90-day: AUC 0.684, churn 7.1%.
- The 60-day label reads seasonal lulls as churn. Led to dec-csb-90d-window.
