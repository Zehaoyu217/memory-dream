# Accounts under 6 months churn at 11.2%, twice the rate of older accounts
- Under 6 months: 14% of SMB accounts and about a quarter of all churners.
- 6 months and over: churn 5.6%. Basis: ds-csb-churn-labels on the training snapshots, 90-day window.
- Tenure is a real driver, but the onboarding playbook already covers that group, which is why the floor was chosen (dec-csb-tenure-floor).
