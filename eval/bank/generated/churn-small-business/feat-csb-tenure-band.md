# feat-csb-tenure-band: five tenure bands, the under-6-month band carries the highest risk
- Bands: 0 to 5, 6 to 11, 12 to 23, 24 to 47 and 48+ months.
- Under 6 months: churn 11.2%, against 5.6% for 6 months and over (fnd-csb-tenure-cut).
- Used in csb-lr-base, csb-xgb-v1 and csb-xgb-v2.
