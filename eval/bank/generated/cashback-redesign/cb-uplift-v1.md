# cb-uplift-v1: T-learner predicting incremental monthly spend per account from the tier pilot
Two outcome models (tier A cell vs control) on spend level, grocery share and tenure. The difference is scored per account.
Uplift@20% is 1.9x the average incremental spend on a 30% split of the pilot (n=16,200). Used to rank accounts for tier offers in cb-sim-v1.
