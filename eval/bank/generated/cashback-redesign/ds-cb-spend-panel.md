# `cb.spend_panel` is monthly spend per account for 612,000 active accounts, 2024-07 to 2026-06
Built by `cb_spend_panel.sql` from `cards.txn_daily`, purchases only, with refunds netted in the month. Owner: finance analytics, with data-eng-cards (Tomas Rivera).
Active = at least one purchase in the month. Average monthly spend $1,240, median $760; the top 5% of accounts hold 31% of spend.
Refreshed monthly after close.
