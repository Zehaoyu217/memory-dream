# Finding: grocery-heavy accounts respond at elasticity 0.38, which explains most of the middle-tier response
Within the banded model, accounts with grocery share above 30% respond at 0.38, against 0.21 pooled. Grocery is 22% of book spend.
Most of the 1.5% tier's response comes from these accounts, so the middle tier is cheap to offer to them.
Source: `cb.spend_panel` by merchant group, pilot cells.
