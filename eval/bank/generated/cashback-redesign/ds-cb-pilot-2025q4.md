# `cb-pilot-2025q4` is a 3-cell randomized pilot, 18,000 accounts per cell, Sep 15 to Dec 14 2025
Cells: control (flat 1.0%), flat 1.25%, and tier A (1.0% base, 1.5% above $1,000, 2.0% above $3,000). Randomized by account ID; no crossover.
Outcomes are monthly spend and rewards accrual per account, from `cb.spend_panel` and `cb.rewards_ledger`.
Known gap: 3.1% of accounts closed or went dormant during the pilot and are kept with zero spend.
