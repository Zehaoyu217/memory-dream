# exp-204: elasticity falls with spend: 0.31 below $1,000, 0.14 at $1,000-3,000, 0.05 above $3,000
Same pilot data as exp-203, split by six-month spend band.
Elasticities: 0.31 below $1,000, 0.14 for $1,000-3,000, 0.05 above $3,000. The top band barely responds.
Implication: a flat rate pays most of its cost to high spenders who do not respond. Tiers aimed at the middle band can be cheaper. Feeds cb-elasticity-v2.
