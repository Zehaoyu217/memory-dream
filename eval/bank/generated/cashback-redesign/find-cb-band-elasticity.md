# Finding: with banded elasticity, tier A beats flat 1.25%, +$0.95 vs +$0.70 per account per month
The pooled elasticity hid that high spenders barely respond, so tier A's top rate is mostly cost. A rate that pays at the middle band is cheaper.
Re-run on the pilot cells with cb-elasticity-v2: tier A +$0.95, flat 1.25% +$0.70 (per account per month, after rewards cost).
Caveat: 3-month pilot; the top band has about 90 days of data.
