# `cb.rewards_ledger` accrues rewards per account-month and reconciles to the GL within 0.4%
Accrual per account-month from the rewards engine, with redemptions and bonus categories kept separate. Built on `cards.accounts_monthly` and the rewards engine export.
Reconciled against the general ledger for 2025-01 to 2025-05: the difference is 0.4% of rewards cost, all timing.
Every cost number in this project is a % of spend unless stated in $.
