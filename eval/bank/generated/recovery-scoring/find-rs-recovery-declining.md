# Recovery is declining by vintage: 2024H1 recovers 7.1% of balance against 9.8% for 2023, so the flat finding is wrong
exp-305 shows the 2024H1 vintage recovering 7.1% of balance within 12 months, against 9.8% for the 2023 vintages.
The earlier flat reading used only the 2023 vintages and missed the decline.
Placement values set on the flat assumption are too high for 2024 vintages; the cutoff decision uses the lower figure.
