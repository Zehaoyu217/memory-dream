# Charged-off accounts from 2023-01 to 2024-06 form the recovery base, with 12-month windows already matured
Source: `cards.accounts_monthly` charge-off events, 2023-01 to 2024-06. n=38,700 charge-offs; median balance at charge-off $2,140.
Only vintages whose 12-month recovery window has closed are included. 2024-07 onward is excluded until its windows mature.
Owner: data-eng-cards (Tomas Rivera). Charge-off date is the day the account is written off, not the 180-DPD date.
