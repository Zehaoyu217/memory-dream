# feat-rs-bureau-postco-v1 is the bureau score at charge-off and six months after, with the change
Features: bureau score at charge-off, bureau score six months after, and the difference.
Source: `bureau.scores_monthly` via ds-rs-bureau-postco.
Accounts with a score that rose after charge-off recover at about 1.4 times the rate of accounts whose score fell.
