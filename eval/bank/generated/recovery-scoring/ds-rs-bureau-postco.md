# bureau.scores_monthly from 0 to 12 months after charge-off, with 88% coverage
Join on account to `bureau.scores_monthly` (credit-data) at charge-off month and in each of the following 12 months.
Coverage 88%. Accounts with no bureau record after charge-off are scored with the segment median and flagged.
Post-charge-off bureau scores are available at placement, so they can be used at placement time.
