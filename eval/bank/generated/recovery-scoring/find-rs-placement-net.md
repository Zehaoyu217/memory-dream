# The placement cutoff that maximises total net recovery is predicted probability 0.18, not 0.10 or 0.25
exp-307: net recovery peaks at cutoff 0.18 at $1.94M, against $1.71M at 0.10 (too many low-yield placements) and $1.62M at 0.25 (too few placements).
Result holds only with the 25% commission; a lower commission would move the optimum down.
