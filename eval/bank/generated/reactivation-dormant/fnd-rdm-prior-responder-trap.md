# Prior responders look best on raw response but show 0.2pp uplift: they would have come back anyway
- Raw response of prior responders is 9.8%, against 2.7% for everyone else (waves 1 and 2).
- Their uplift against the 10% control is 0.2pp, so most of their response is not caused by the send.
- Targeting by response propensity sends to them first and wastes the budget.
