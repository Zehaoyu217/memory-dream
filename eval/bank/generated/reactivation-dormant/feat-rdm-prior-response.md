# feat-rdm-prior-response: responded to an earlier reactivation wave
- Response within 60 days in waves 1 or 2. About 14% of the dormant pool has it.
- Raw response of prior responders is 9.8%, against 2.7% for everyone else.
- Strong raw predictor, but its uplift is close to zero (fnd-rdm-prior-responder-trap).
