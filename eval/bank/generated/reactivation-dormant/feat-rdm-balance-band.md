# feat-rdm-balance-band: four residual-balance bands
- Bands: zero, under $500, $500 to $2,999, and $3,000 or more.
- Zero-balance accounts respond at about a third of the rate of the $500-plus bands.
- Used in rdm-propensity-lr, rdm-uplift-v1 and rdm-uplift-v2.
