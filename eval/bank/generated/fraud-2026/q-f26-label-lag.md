# Are 2026 CNP chargeback labels mature enough to evaluate on recent months?
Labels arrive up to 60 days late; 97% of 2026 CNP fraud labels arrive within 60 days, median lag 34 days.
Evaluating a month before its labels mature treats immature frauds as legit and understates AUC by about 0.02.
Answer in fraud26-find-label-bias: wait for maturity or drop the young labels.
