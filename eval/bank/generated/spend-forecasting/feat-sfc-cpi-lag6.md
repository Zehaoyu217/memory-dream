# feat-sfc-cpi-lag6: six-month lag of year-on-year CPI
Year-on-year CPI at a six-month lag, applied to all segments.
Adds 0.1pp at 1 month, 0.4pp at 3 months and 0.6pp at 6 months (fnd-sfc-macro-weak).
Used by sfc-lgbm-v2 and sfc-hier-v3.
