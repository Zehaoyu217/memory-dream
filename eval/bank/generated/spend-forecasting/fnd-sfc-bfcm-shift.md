# The Black Friday peak moved from December into November in 2023 and 2024
For these customer segments, the spend peak of the Black Friday and Cyber week period moved from December to November between 2023 and 2024.
Fixed-date calendar terms therefore put the peak in the wrong month for 2024 and 2025.
The shifted date set is used in feat-sfc-season-v2.
