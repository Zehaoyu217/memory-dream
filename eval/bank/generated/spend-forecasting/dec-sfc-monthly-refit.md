# Decision: refit segment models on the first working day of each month
The models are refit on the first working day of each month using actuals to the prior month-end.
The forecast is frozen at 17:00 on day 2 and compared with actuals on day 5.
Student forecasts carry a wider band until the semester-timing feature exists.
