# Does macro data help the segment spend forecast at 3- and 6-month horizons?
Open question for spend-forecasting until Aug 2025. At one month ahead, a six-month CPI lag adds little.
Answered by exp-204: the CPI lag cuts 3-month MAPE from 6.8% to 6.4% and 6-month MAPE from 8.9% to 8.3%.
Eval set is sfc-bt-2024 (rolling origins across 2024, six customer segments).
See fnd-sfc-macro-weak for the conclusion.
