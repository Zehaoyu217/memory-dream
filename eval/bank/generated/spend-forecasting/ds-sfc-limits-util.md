# Monthly credit-limit utilisation per account, 2023-01 to 2025-08
Balance divided by limit per account per month, from `cards.limits_history` (owner credit-data).
Covers about 1.2M active accounts; accounts with a limit change in the month are excluded.
Used only by feat-sfc-util-v1, for the Rewards segment.
