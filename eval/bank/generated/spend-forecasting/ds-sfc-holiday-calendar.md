# retail-calendar-v3: fixed dates and shopping events
Calendar of fixed dates and shopping events (back-to-school, year-end sales, Black Friday and Cyber week, Boxing Day).
Event dates are set per year; Black Friday and Cyber week are stored as a separate date set because their timing moved in 2023 and 2024.
Maintained by the forecasting team; read by feat-sfc-season-v2.
