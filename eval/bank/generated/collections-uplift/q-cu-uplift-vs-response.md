# Does an uplift model choose better early-collections calls than a response model?
A response model ranks accounts by the chance they pay if called. An uplift model ranks by the extra chance a call adds over no call.
Proposed test: the randomised pilot (ds-cu-treat-log), comparing promise-to-pay uplift@20% for each ranking.
Status 2025-05-16: answered yes on pilot data. See exp-102 and find-cu-response-misranks.
