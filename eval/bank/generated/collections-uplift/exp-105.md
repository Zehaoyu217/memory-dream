# exp-105: T-learner with isotonic calibration (uplift v2) reaches uplift@20% of 0.034 and lower Brier on holdout-2025q4
Holdout-2025q4, n=31,200 accounts. cu-uplift-v2 (T-learner, isotonic calibration of each arm): uplift@20% 0.034, Brier 0.181. cu-uplift-v1 on the same split: uplift@20% 0.031, Brier 0.196.
Calibration matters for the call-budget rule: uncalibrated arms overstate the top decile.
Source: `collections_uplift/exp105_calib.ipynb`, run 2025-12-09.
