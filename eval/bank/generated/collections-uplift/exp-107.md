# exp-107: per-contact economics put SMS at 9% of call cost while capturing 83% of call uplift in the top 20%
Economics run on the top 20% by cu-uplift-v3 in the collections-uplift-oot-2026h1 scoring population (n=5,690 accounts, 1-29 DPD).
Call uplift 0.036 at $4.10 per contact; SMS uplift 0.030 at $0.37 per contact. Uplift ratio 0.83, cost ratio 0.09.
Cost per incremental PTP: call about $114, SMS about $12.
Caveat: the SMS and call uplifts come from separate arms, not one randomised test. The randomised channel test is in collections-channel-mix.
Source: `collections_uplift/exp107_econ.ipynb`, run 2026-03-18.
