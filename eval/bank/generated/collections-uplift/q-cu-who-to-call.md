# Which 1-29 DPD accounts should get an outbound call rather than an SMS?
Open question from Raj Patel's early-collections review. Current practice calls every account at 3 DPD and again at 8 DPD regardless of risk, at about $4.10 per contact.
Options: rank by response likelihood (cu-resp-v1), rank by the incremental effect of a call (uplift models), or send SMS to everyone and call only the top decile.
The answer depends on q-cu-uplift-vs-response, which carries the head-to-head test.
