# SMS captures 83% of call uplift at about 9% of the cost per contact, for 1-29 DPD accounts
From exp-107: calling the top 20% by cu-uplift-v3 yields uplift 0.036; SMS to the same accounts yields 0.030. Cost per contact is $4.10 for a call and $0.37 for SMS.
Per incremental promise-to-pay, SMS costs about $12 and a call about $114.
Holds for 1-7 DPD. For 8-29 DPD the gap is wider and is revisited in collections-channel-mix.
