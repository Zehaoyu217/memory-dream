# feat-xs-call-recent: any call to the centre in the 90 days before offer open
Binary flag from `callcenter.calls`. Strongly associated with opening, but mostly through the reasons people call (fees, limit disputes).
Excluded from xs-gbm-v2; used only in the call uplift study (xs-call-uplift-v1).
