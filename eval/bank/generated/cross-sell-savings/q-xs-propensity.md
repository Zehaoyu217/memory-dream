# Q: Which cardholders are most likely to open a savings account after a card-side offer?
Owner: Priya Nair (onboarding). The offer is shown in the card app and by email; a savings account opened within 30 days counts as a response.
Baseline open rate is 2.9% of eligible cardholders in `sav.offer_log`. Goal: rank the book so the offer goes to the top deciles only.
Success is measured as open rate in the top decile on a holdout.
