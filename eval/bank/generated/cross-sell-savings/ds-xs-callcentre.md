# `callcenter.calls` joined to offers: 14% of cardholders called in the 90 days before an offer
Calls from `callcenter.calls` (cx-analytics), flagged if any call falls in the 90 days before offer open. 14% of cardholders in the log had one.
Calls are self-selected: people call about fees, disputes and limits. The naive comparison between called and not called is biased.
