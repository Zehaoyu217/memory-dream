# KS (Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistic)
KS is the maximum separation between the cumulative score distributions of goods and bads: max over score of |cum_good - cum_bad|, reported on a 0-100 scale. In scorecards a KS above 40 is considered strong.
Example: a KS of 42 means the best cut separates 42 points of cumulative distribution.
