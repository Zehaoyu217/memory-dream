# MAPE (mean absolute percentage error)
MAPE is the mean of |actual - forecast| / actual across all accounts or months, reported as a percentage. Zero-balance accounts are included and count as 0% error.
Used for balance forecasting in the monthly model review.
Example: a 2.1% MAPE on the Q4 2024 balance forecast.
