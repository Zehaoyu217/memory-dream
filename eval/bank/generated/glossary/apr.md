# APR (annual percentage rate)
Purchase APR is the nominal annual rate charged on carried purchase balances: the prime rate plus a fixed margin. New accounts opened in 2024 carried a margin of 12.5 points. Daily interest is APR / 365 x balance.
Example: prime 7.50% + 12.5 points = 20.00% purchase APR, a daily rate of 0.0548%.
