# book
The book is total card receivables in $: the sum of current balances across all open accounts at month-end, from `cards.accounts_monthly`, including commercial cards.
"% of book" means a segment's balance divided by the total book.
Example: Q4 2024 book $4.1bn; prime segment $2.6bn, so 63% of book.
