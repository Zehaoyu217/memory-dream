# recall@k
recall@k is the share of the top-k ranked accounts that are positives, divided by k. It is the number the weekly KPI deck uses to compare targeting models.
Example: top 10% of 500,000 accounts is 50,000 accounts; 2,300 positives in that group gives recall@10% of 4.6%.
