# CLI (credit limit increase)
A CLI is an offer to raise a cardholder's credit line. The offer amount is the increment in $ added to the current limit, accepted by the cardholder at the offer. Offers come from `offers.tier_matrix`.
Example: current limit $5,000 and a CLI offer of $1,500 gives a new limit of $6,500.
