# churn
Churn is an account that closes. A churned account is one closed inside the label window, using the closure date in `cards.accounts_monthly`. Churn labels are stored in `cards.churn_labels`.
Monthly churn rate = accounts closed in the month / opening accounts.
Example: 1.1% monthly churn in Sept 2024.
