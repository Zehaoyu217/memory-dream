# Q: Which cardholders accept a balance-transfer offer, and can we rank them before mailing?
Owner: Maya Ortiz (retention). Acceptance = a BT booked within 45 days of offer open, from `bt.offer_log`.
Baseline: 4.3% of exposed accounts accept per wave, about 2.3% of the book.
The mailer reaches 30% of the book per wave, so the score needs to rank the top third well, not be calibrated.
Success criterion agreed with Maya: lift at top 30% of at least 1.8x on a holdout.
