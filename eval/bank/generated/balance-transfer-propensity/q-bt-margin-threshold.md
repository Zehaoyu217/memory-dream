# Q: What 12-month net margin per accepted BT should decide who gets mailed?
A BT is worth mailing only if its 12-month net interest margin after promo fee and expected loss is positive.
Ben Carter (finance) asked for the threshold in $ per account, not in AUC.
Open: break-even depends on promo length (6 vs 12 months) and on balances surviving past promo expiry.
