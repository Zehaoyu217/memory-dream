# Matched control sample: 14,020 enrolled accounts matched to 56,080 controls at 4:1 on six covariates
Matched sample built from the eligible pool: 14,020 enrolled accounts matched to 56,080 non-enrolled controls, 4:1 nearest neighbour on the propensity score.
800 enrolled accounts were unmatched and dropped.
Matching covariates: feat-hp-match-covars-v1. Pre-period DPD history: feat-hp-pre-dpd-v1.
