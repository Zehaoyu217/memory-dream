# Decision: default new hardship plans to 6 months where the customer's budget allows
Agreed 2025-06-25 by Raj Patel (collections) and Yuki Tanaka (customer experience).
Six months is the default from July 2025. Three months stays available on request and where budget does not support six.
Plan length is recorded at enrolment so the effect can be tracked.
