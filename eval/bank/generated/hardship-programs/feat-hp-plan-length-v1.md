# feat-hp-plan-length-v1 is the plan length as a dose: 3 months or 6 months, from the enrolment record
Feature: plan length at enrolment (3 or 6 months), plus a flag for plan extended after enrolment.
Source: `collections.cases` plan terms in ds-hp-enrolled.
Split of 2024 enrolments: 4,310 on 6-month plans and 9,710 on 3-month plans, with the remainder on other lengths.
