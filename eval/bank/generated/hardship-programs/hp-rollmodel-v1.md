# hp-rollmodel-v1 is a Markov roll model that turns the hardship effect into a long-run charge-off estimate
Inputs: the transition matrix in ds-hp-roll-matrix, and the enrolment effect from hp-psm-v2 (-4.7pp on the 30-to-60 roll).
Output: long-run charge-off change of -0.9pp per enrolled account (exp-407).
Used for scenario planning only. It is not a forecast of programme cost.
