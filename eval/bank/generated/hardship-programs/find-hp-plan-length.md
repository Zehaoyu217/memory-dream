# Six-month hardship plans roll 5.2pp less than matched controls, against 2.6pp for three-month plans
exp-405 result. The longer plan gives a larger reduction in 30-to-60 rolls.
Caveat: 6-month plans were offered to lower-risk enrolments, which the matching partly controls for but cannot fully remove.
