# exp-402: matched controls roll at 27.1% against 21.0% for enrolled accounts, an adjusted effect of -6.1pp
Matched sample: 14,020 enrolled and 56,080 controls (4:1, hp-psm-v1 on six covariates). Enrolled 21.0%, matched controls 27.1%. Effect -6.1pp (CI -7.4 to -4.8).
The matched controls are riskier than the naive pool, which is why the naive comparison overstated harm.
