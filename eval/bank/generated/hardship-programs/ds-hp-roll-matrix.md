# Monthly roll matrix of DPD buckets, with the 30 to 60 roll as the outcome
Monthly transition matrix over buckets 0, 1-29, 30-59, 60+ and charged off, built from `cards.accounts_monthly` and `cards.payments_daily`.
Outcome used throughout: 30-to-60 roll, the share of accounts at 30-59 DPD that reach 60+ within 30 days.
Eligible non-enrolled pool for the naive comparison: 402,500 accounts at 30-59 DPD in 2024.
