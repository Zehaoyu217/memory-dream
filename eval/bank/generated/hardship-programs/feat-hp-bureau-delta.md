# feat-hp-bureau-delta is the bureau score change over the 3 months before enrolment
Feature: bureau score at enrolment minus the score three months earlier, plus a flag for a drop of 40 points or more.
Source: `bureau.scores_monthly`, as of the index month.
Enrolled accounts show a 40-point bureau drop in 26% of cases, against 9% of matched controls.
