# exp-405: 6-month plans cut the 30-to-60 roll by 5.2pp against 2.6pp for 3-month plans, both against matched controls
Matched controls from hp-psm-v1. 6-month plans (n=4,310): effect -5.2pp. 3-month plans (n=9,710): effect -2.6pp.
The dose gap is larger than the sampling error. The 6-month effect is the basis for dec-hp-6mo-default.
