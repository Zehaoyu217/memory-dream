# Decision: page on score PSI at 0.20 or more; feature PSI at 0.25 is diagnostic only; warn at 0.10
Replaces the 2025 rule of paging on either feature or score PSI at 0.25. Agreed on 2026-02-02 with Raj Patel and Lena Fischer after two weeks of live shadow pages.
Basis: exp-207, 18 of 19 incidents caught with 3 false pages. Warn at 0.10 still goes to the dashboard only.
