# exp-202: PSI threshold sweep on 19 labelled incidents: page at 0.25 catches 16 with 4 false pages
Swept warn and page bars for feature PSI on the 19 labelled incidents (2024-07 to 2025-02).
Warn at 0.10 caught all 19 but averaged 37 warnings per month. Page at 0.25 caught 16 of 19, with 4 false pages. Page at 0.30 caught 11 of 19, with 1 false page.
Chosen as the starting point: 0.25 page, since the cost of a missed incident was higher than a false page.
