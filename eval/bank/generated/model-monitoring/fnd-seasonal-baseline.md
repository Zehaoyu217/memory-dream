# A same-month-last-year reference removes most seasonal false alarms on the limit model
Summer 2025 alarms fell from 7 to 2 (exp-205), and the Nov-Dec 2024 backtest went from 9 to 0 (exp-206).
The two remaining real alarms in summer were policy changes, which is the kind of drift the monitor should catch.
Basis for dec-mm-seasonal-reference.
