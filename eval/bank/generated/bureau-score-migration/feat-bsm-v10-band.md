# feat-bsm-v10-band: ten v10 bands aligned to the v8 cutoff bands 1-9
Bands are the isotonic-mapped v10 score cut at the points where the v8 bands change, so each v10 band maps to one v8 band.
Band cut points are fixed until the next map refit.
Used in bsm-cutoff-logit and the 2026 cutover.
