# 14% of revolvers become transactors within 12 months, so revolver is not a permanent label
Twelve-month roll rate from revolver to transactor is 14%; to minimum-payer 9% (exp-405).
Segment labels are therefore refreshed monthly, not fixed at onboarding.
Retention offers should key on the current label.
