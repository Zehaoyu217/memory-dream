# How many payment-behaviour segments are there, and why that number?
Open at the start of the project. Silhouette favours four clusters, but four merges the minimum-payers with the dormant accounts.
Working answer by Aug 2025: six, from the kmeans run (pbs-kmeans-v1). That answer was revised in Jan 2026 to five (fnd-pbs-five-segments).
