# exp-402: GMM with five components is more stable than kmeans with six (seed ARI 0.74 vs 0.58)
GMM k=5 against kmeans k=6 on the same 200k sample.
Silhouette 0.39 (GMM) against 0.36 (kmeans); seed-to-seed ARI 0.74 against 0.58.
The stability gap is the main reason for moving to GMM.
