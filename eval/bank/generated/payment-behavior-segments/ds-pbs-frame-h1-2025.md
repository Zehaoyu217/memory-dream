# Active-account frame at 2025-06-30: 1.38M accounts aged six months or more
The frame is accounts open at 2025-06-30 for at least six months, excluding closed and charged-off accounts; 1.38M accounts.
A 200k random sample is used for clustering; the full frame is used for the roll-rate tests (exp-405).
Frame rebuilt on the first working day of each month from then on.
