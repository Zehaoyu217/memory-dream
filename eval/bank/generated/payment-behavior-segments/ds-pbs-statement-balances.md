# Statement balance, minimum due and credit limit per account per month, Jul 2024 to Jun 2025
Statement-cycle fields from `cards.accounts_monthly`: statement balance, minimum due and limit, for the same 12 months as ds-pbs-payments-12m.
Used as the denominator for the pay ratio and for the minimum-payment flag.
Accounts with a limit change in the window are kept but flagged.
