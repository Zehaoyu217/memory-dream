# Payment timing separates minimum-payers who pay on the last day from early payers
Adding feat-pbs-timing-v2 raises seed ARI from 0.74 to 0.81 (exp-403).
The minimum-payers who pay on day 21 to 25 and those who pay early look alike on amount alone.
Timing is therefore the feature that makes the minimum-payer segment a real group.
