# Payments per account per month, Jul 2024 to Jun 2025
Monthly payment totals per account from `cards.payments_daily`, aggregated to the month, for the 12 months to June 2025.
Covers the 1.38M accounts in ds-pbs-frame-h1-2025; the clustering uses a 200k sample.
Source for feat-pbs-pay-ratio-v1, feat-pbs-minpay-flag, feat-pbs-cash-share and feat-pbs-timing-v2.
