# feat-pbs-interest-paid: interest charged and paid over 12 months, scaled by limit
Sum of interest charged and paid over 12 months, divided by the credit limit.
Adds signal for the revolver group that the pay ratio cannot separate from convenience users.
Used in pbs-gmm-v2 and pbs-gmm-v3.
