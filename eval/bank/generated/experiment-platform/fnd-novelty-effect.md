# Campaign lift is about twice as large in week 1 as in weeks 3 and 4, so two-week readouts overstate the effect
exp-506 on MK-22: week 1 lift 6.2 percent, weeks 3 and 4 lift 2.9 percent. A two-week readout averages both and sits above the steady state.
Answers q-ep-novelty-length: readouts for consumer campaigns should run at least four weeks, with the first two weeks reported separately.
