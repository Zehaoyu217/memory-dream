# exp-503: A/A simulation with daily unadjusted peeks gives a 14 percent false positive rate against a nominal 5 percent
Ran 500 A/A simulations with daily looks at p below 0.05, no correction. Each simulated test ran for 14 days.
False positive rate with daily unadjusted looks: 14 percent. With one look at day 14: 5 percent, as nominal.
