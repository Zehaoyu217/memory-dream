# ep.exposures: logged exposure events, 61 percent of assigned onboarding units exposed in median
Each row is an exposure event that the app logs when the unit sees the test. Exposure rate in onboarding tests is 61 percent of assigned units (median across tests).
Marketing tests have lower exposure rates because campaigns reach only part of the base.
Analyses use exposed units by default, with the assigned-unit intent-to-treat result reported next to them.
