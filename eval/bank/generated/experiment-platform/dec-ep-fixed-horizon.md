# Decision: readouts happen once, at the planned end; early stops need a sequential boundary
Daily looks are still shown on the monitoring dashboard, but no stop decision is taken from them.
A test ends at its planned horizon with one readout. Early stops are allowed only with a pre-registered sequential boundary, which the platform computes.
