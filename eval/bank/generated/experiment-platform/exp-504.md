# exp-504: a 28-day pre-period with a 7-day lag gives 27 percent variance reduction against 24 percent for 14 days
Compared pre-period windows on OB-19 (180,000 units, spend primary).
28 days ending 7 days before assignment: 27 percent. The v1 14-day window ending on assignment day: 24 percent. The lag costs little and removes possible contamination.
