# Daily unadjusted peeking pushes the false positive rate to 14 percent
exp-503: 500 A/A simulations, 14-day tests. Daily looks at p below 0.05 gave 14 percent false positives; one look at the end gave 5 percent.
Any readout rule with early stops has to correct for multiple looks. Basis for dec-ep-fixed-horizon.
