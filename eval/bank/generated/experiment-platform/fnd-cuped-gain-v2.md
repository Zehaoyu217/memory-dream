# CUPED v2 cuts variance by a median 27 percent, up from 18 percent with v1
exp-505: median 27 percent with ep-cuped-v2 across 12 finished tests, against 18 percent for v1 on the same tests.
The gain is largest on sparse-spend tests, which were the weakest under v1.
