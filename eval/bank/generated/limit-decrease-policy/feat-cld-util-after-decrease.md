# feat-cld-util-after-decrease: utilisation against the new limit for 60 days after a cut
Utilisation is balance over the new, lower limit, averaged over the first 60 days after the decrease.
Accounts above 0.95 for 60 days show 30+ DPD roll 2.2x the cohort average.
Feeds cld-complaint-logit and feat-cld-payment-shock.
