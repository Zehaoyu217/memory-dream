# ds-cld-collections-roll: roll outcomes for decrease-cohort accounts from collections.cases
Joins the decrease cohort to `collections.cases` to get the first case opened within 90 days and its stage.
Hardship-programme accounts are flagged from the case reason, 6.8% of the cohort.
Used for the hardship question and for roll-rate evaluation in cld-gbm-v1.
