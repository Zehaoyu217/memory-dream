# exp-201: pilot of 25% cuts on bands C and D gives 4.6 complaints per 1,000 versus 1.9 baseline (2.4x)
February 2025 wave, 18,600 accounts in band C or D with a 25% cut.
Complaints in the 30 days after the cut: 4.6 per 1,000 account-months against the 1.9 baseline (2.4x).
Notice was sent on the day of the cut in this pilot; exp-202 tests a 30-day notice.
