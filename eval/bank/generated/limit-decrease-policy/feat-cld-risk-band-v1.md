# feat-cld-risk-band-v1: four risk bands from bureau v8 score and utilisation
Bands A to D from the v8 bureau score and current utilisation: band D is score below 620 with utilisation above 90%.
Band C is score 620 to 659 or utilisation above 80%; A and B are the rest, split on score 700.
First cut rule: 25% decrease for band C, 35% for band D.
