# feat-cld-payment-shock: change in minimum-payment ratio after a decrease
Compares the minimum payment as a share of balance 2 cycles before and 2 cycles after the decrease.
Shock above 0.02 of balance marks accounts likely to miss payments; 9% of the cohort.
First used in cld-gbm-v3 (2026-05-18).
