# exp-202: 30-day notice before the cut gives 3.1 complaints per 1,000 versus 4.6 for same-day notice
Test on 9,300 band C and D accounts per arm, March 2025 wave; 30-day notice arm versus same-day arm.
Complaints in 30 days: 3.1 per 1,000 for the notice arm, 4.6 for same-day.
Roll rates were not different between the arms at 90 days (difference under 0.3 points).
