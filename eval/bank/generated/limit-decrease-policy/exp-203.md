# exp-203: against the 5% randomised control, complaint rate is 2.3x raw but 1.1x after matching on call reason
Compared decreased accounts (22,150) with cld-control-2025h2 (41,380, no decrease).
Raw complaint rate ratio 2.3x; after matching the call-reason mix of the two groups, the ratio is 1.1x.
Most of the raw gap is from the mix of reasons, not from the cut itself.
