# Analyst label noise caps measured precision near 95-96%
Two-analyst kappa is 0.71 on 400 double-labelled disputes; 8% of disagreements are friendly-fraud vs merchant-error calls.
Label noise means no model can show much more than about 96% measured precision on these labels.
Keep the 95% target, but read precision claims above 96% with suspicion.
