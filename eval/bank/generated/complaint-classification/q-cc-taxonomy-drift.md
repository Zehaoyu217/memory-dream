# Did the July 2025 billing-code split degrade classification of billing complaints?
Taxonomy v3 splits billing error into fee and amount codes (ds-cc-taxonomy-v3). A first check on the July tickets shows a model trained on v2 codes scores 0.52 F1 on the split pair.
Open until the split pair is relabelled and re-measured (exp-205).
