# `complaints.labels_qa` holds 9,000 tickets labelled by three reviewers, June to August 2025
Each ticket was coded independently by three reviewers, then adjudicated by a senior coder. Table owner complaints-ops.
Fleiss kappa 0.82 across reviewers. Adjudication changed 9 percent of first-pass labels.
Training uses adjudicated labels only. Holdout labels are kept out of every training run.
