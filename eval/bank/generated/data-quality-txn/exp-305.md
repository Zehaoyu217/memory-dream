# exp-305: dispute rate per 10k approved txns is 11.8 with duplicates and 11.6 after dedupe (2025-Q3)
Joined cards.txn_daily to cards.disputes for 2025-Q3. Raw rows give 11.8 disputes per 10,000 approved transactions. Deduped rows, first arrival kept, give 11.6.
The 0.2 gap is the duplicates inflating the denominator and the numerator together, which is why the rate moves at all.
