# After the batch moved to 04:30 in October 2025, late partitions fell to 1.6 percent
The upstream batch start moved from 05:00 to 04:30 in October 2025. Late share was 1.6 percent over 2025-Q4 and January 2026 (n=118 partitions).
The Monday skew is smaller but still present. The retry-driven late partitions are now the bigger share of the remainder.
