# Deduping moves the dispute rate from 11.8 to 11.6 per 10,000 approved transactions
exp-305 on 2025-Q3: raw rows give 11.8 disputes per 10,000 approved transactions, deduped rows give 11.6.
The change is small but real, and it is in the direction fraud-ops expects if duplicates were inflating the denominator.
