# About 4.1 percent of partitions were late in 2025-Q1, mostly on Mondays and month-starts
exp-301: 4 of 98 partitions arrived after 10:00 on D+1, and most of them fell on Mondays or the first business day of the month.
The pattern points to weekend loads. This finding was later superseded by fnd-late-share-2026.
