# Does intent routing bring the blind-transfer rate below 15 percent?
Blind transfers are calls moved to another queue without a warm handoff. Baseline 19.2 percent for Jan to Jun 2026, n = 1.02M inbound calls in `callcenter.calls`.
Target is 15 percent or lower, with FCR not dropping below 70.5 percent.
Dispute-queue misroutes are the largest single share of blind transfers (see fnd-cr-threshold).
