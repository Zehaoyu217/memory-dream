# Pilot the router on 10 percent of inbound calls from 2026-10-05
The pilot covers 10 percent of inbound calls, randomised by caller_key, for four weeks.
Success: blind-transfer rate at or below 15 percent (baseline 19.2 percent) and FCR at or above 70.5 percent.
Rollback trigger: FCR below 70.5 percent on three consecutive days.
