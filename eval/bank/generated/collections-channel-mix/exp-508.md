# exp-508: SMS-then-call escalation on day 5 gives 11.2% promise-to-pay at $25.30 per promise, against $37.60 for call-first
Wave-2 SMS-then-call arm (n=17,900): PTP 11.2% within 14 days. Cost per account is $0.37 for SMS plus $4.10 for a call to the 60% who do not respond, about $2.83. Cost per promise $2.83 divided by 0.112, about $25.30.
Call-first: $4.10 per account divided by 10.9%, about $37.60 per promise.
