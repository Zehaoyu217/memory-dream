# Email open rate of 61% overstates engagement: click rate is 2.2%, so opens are not a usable engagement signal
From ds-ch-email-engagement: the reported open rate is 61% against a click rate of 2.2%. The gap is consistent with image pre-fetching by mail clients.
Implication: email open cannot be used to score channel response or to justify keeping email in the plan.
