# Wave-1 randomised channel test: 86,000 accounts at 1-29 DPD across SMS, call, email and control, Jul-Aug 2025
Four arms: SMS 28,400, call 28,300, email 14,500, control 14,800. Total n=86,000.
Outcome: promise-to-pay or payment within 14 days of the first contact. Randomised at account level, stratified by DPD bucket.
Source: `collections.cases` joined to `callcenter.calls` and the SMS and email logs.
