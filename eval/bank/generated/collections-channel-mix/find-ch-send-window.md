# SMS sent in the morning window gets 0.8pp more promise-to-pay than afternoon sends, in wave 1
On the wave-1 SMS arm (n=28,400), morning sends (08-11) produce promise-to-pay at 11.9% against 11.1% for afternoon sends (14-18).
The difference is inside the CI, so treat it as a tiebreak, not a rule.
