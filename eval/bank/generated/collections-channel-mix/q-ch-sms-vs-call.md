# Does SMS capture enough of a call's lift to replace calls in early collections?
Raj Patel's question. Calls cost about 11 times SMS per contact, so the question is whether the promise-to-pay lift from a call is worth the difference.
Test: a randomised wave with SMS, call, email and control arms on 1-29 DPD accounts (ds-ch-ab-wave1).
Earlier separate-arm economics are in collections-uplift exp-107.
