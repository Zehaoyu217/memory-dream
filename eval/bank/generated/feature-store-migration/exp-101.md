# exp-101: first parity run, all 214 legacy vs store features, 250k sample: 13 features fail the bar
Ran the parity harness on fsm_parity_sample_2025h2 (250,000 account-months, 2025-04 to 2025-09) for every migrated feature.
Result: 201 of 214 features have at least 99 percent of rows within 0.5 percent.
The 13 failures: 9 windowed month-end features on local-time windows, payment lag v1 (3.1 percent of rows off), and 3 bureau features with a file-date join.
