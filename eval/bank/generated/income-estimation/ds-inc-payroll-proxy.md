# ds-inc-payroll-proxy: recurring ACH inflows on linked bank sources, 38% of accounts with a cadence
Inflows from ACH payment sources in `cards.payments_daily`, grouped by source account and amount band.
A source with 3 or more credits on a 14-16 day or monthly cadence in 3 months is a payroll candidate.
Coverage is 38% of booked accounts; 62% have no detectable cadence.
