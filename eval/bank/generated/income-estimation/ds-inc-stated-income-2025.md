# ds-inc-stated-income-2025: stated income on 61,300 applications, Jan to Jun 2025
Stated annual income from the application form, stored in `crm.contacts` and joined to `cards.accounts_monthly` for booked accounts.
Booked accounts only, 61,300 rows; median stated income $58,400.
Stated income is the training target for inc-linear-baseline and inc-gbm-v1 until the verified sample exists.
