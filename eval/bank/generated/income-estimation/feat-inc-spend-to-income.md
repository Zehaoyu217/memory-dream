# feat-inc-spend-to-income: 12-month card spend divided by the income estimate
Ratio of 12-month card spend to the current income estimate, capped at 3.
Ratios above 1.2 mark spend the estimate does not support; 17% of booked accounts.
Included in inc-gbm-v1 and every income model since.
