# feat-inc-rent-flag: recurring rent-like outflow flag from ACH and card payees
Flag is 1 when a payee repeats monthly at a stable amount for 6 months and the category is rent-like.
Share of flagged accounts is 21%; rent-like outflow sits at a median of 27% of the estimated monthly income.
Used in inc-gbm-v1 and later.
