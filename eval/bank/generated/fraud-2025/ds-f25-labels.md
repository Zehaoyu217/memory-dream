# cards.fraud_labels arrives with a median lag of 21 days in 2025
Source: `cards.fraud_labels` for the same CNP window as ds-f25-txn-2024-2025.
Median label lag 21 days, 95% of fraud labels within 45 days; the 2025 pipeline uses a 45-day maturity cut.
Later labels are not used for training; the 2026 lag is much longer (see fraud-2026).
