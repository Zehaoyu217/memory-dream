# 180-day revenue per account from cards.txn_daily and cards.payments_daily: median $164, mean $287
- Revenue = interchange plus interest plus fees, summed over 180 days after the offer date.
- Interest is about 58% of revenue; the mean is pulled up by the top value decile.
- Used as the revenue term in ROI, before the credit-loss netting that feat-ror-value-band-v2 adds.
