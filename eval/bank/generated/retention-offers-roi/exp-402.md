# exp-402: retention uplift falls from 2.3pp with no prior offers to 0.4pp with three or more
- Uplift = 60-day churn reduction against control, by offers in the prior 12 months.
- 0 offers: 2.3pp. 1 offer: 2.1pp. 2 offers: 1.4pp. 3 or more: 0.4pp.
- Basis: July 2025 wave in ds-ror-redemptions, same 10% control within each bucket.
