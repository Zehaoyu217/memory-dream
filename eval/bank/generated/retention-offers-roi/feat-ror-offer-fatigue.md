# feat-ror-offer-fatigue: retention offers received in the trailing 12 months
- Four levels: 0, 1, 2, and 3 or more offers.
- Built from the offer history in ds-ror-redemptions.
- Strongly tied to uplift: 2.3pp with none, 0.4pp with three or more (exp-402).
