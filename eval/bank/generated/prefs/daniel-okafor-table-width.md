# Daniel Okafor caps alert-review tables at eight columns and sends wider data to a CSV
- Tables in the weekly alert review have at most eight columns.
- Wider data goes to a CSV in the review folder, named with the holdout and date.
- Reason: tables wider than eight columns were scrolled past in the 2025 reviews, and the ops team missed a threshold change.
