# Raj Patel wants raw case counts printed beside every collections rate
- Collections tables show the rate and the case count together, for example "Roll 30 to 60: 4.1% (n = 318 cases)".
- Raj says a rate on a small roll bucket means nothing without its count, and he has sent back tables that show only the percentage.
- This is the reverse of Maya Ortiz's rule for retention (see prefs/maya-ortiz-retention-units); the two teams keep their own conventions.
