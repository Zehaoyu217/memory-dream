# Omar Haddad checks fair-lending approval rates as a ratio and flags anything below 0.80
- Approval-rate disparity is reported as a ratio of approval rates between groups, using the lowest rate over the highest.
- A ratio below 0.80 (the four-fifths rule) is flagged in the compliance section and footnoted.
- Omar's review checks the ratio on the same population and date window as the deck; a mismatch sends the slide back.
