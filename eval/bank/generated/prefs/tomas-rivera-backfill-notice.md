# Tomás Rivera announces every backfill in data-eng-cards with table, date range and reason
- A backfill is posted before it runs: table name, date range, reason and expected finish time.
- No backfill runs silently, even when it changes no published number.
- Reason: in 2025 a silent backfill of `cards.payments_daily` moved a board number by $4.2m without anyone knowing why.
