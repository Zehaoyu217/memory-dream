# Raj Patel expresses roll rates as % of the prior bucket per cycle, not per month
- A roll rate is the share of accounts moving from one delinquency bucket to the next, measured per billing cycle.
- Monthly roll rates are not shown because the billing cycle is 28 to 31 days and the monthly figure drifts with the calendar.
- Example: "Roll 30 to 60: 4.1% per cycle of accounts in 30-DPD."
