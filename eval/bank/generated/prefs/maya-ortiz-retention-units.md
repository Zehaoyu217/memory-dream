# Maya Ortiz wants retention results as % of book, never raw account counts
- Retention tables and charts from Maya's work are expressed as % of book (accounts open at the start of the cohort month), shown to one decimal place.
- Raw account counts are banned from her retention views because segment book sizes differ widely (Prime 1.9M accounts vs Starter 210k in 2026-01, `cards.accounts_monthly`), so counts make small segments look like they are shrinking.
- If a count is needed for context, it goes in the appendix, not on the chart.
- Example: cohort 2025-07 at month 6 is 88.4% of book (n = 1.2M), not 1,061,000 accounts.
