# Chloe Martin bans pie charts and dual-axis charts in campaign reporting
- No pie or donut charts for campaign mix; a sorted bar chart with direct labels is used instead.
- No dual-axis charts. If two measures are needed, use two small panels sharing an x-axis.
- Small multiples by segment are her default for campaign response: one panel per segment, same y-scale in every panel.
