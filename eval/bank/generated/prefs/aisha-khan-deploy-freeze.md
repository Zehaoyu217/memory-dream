# Aisha Khan blocks model deploys on Friday afternoons; hotfixes need her sign-off
- No model deploys after 14:00 London on Fridays, and none across bank holidays.
- A hotfix that cannot wait for Monday needs Aisha Khan's written sign-off in the release channel before it goes out.
- Reason: a Friday 16:30 deploy of `cli-gbm-v4` in 2025 needed a weekend rollback.
