# Maya Ortiz puts caveats in a "Read this first" line above the chart, not in footnotes
- Each retention chart carries one plain-English line above the plot, starting "Read this first:", with the single biggest caveat.
- Example: "Read this first: cohorts after 2025-09 have fewer than six months of history."
- Footnotes are not used because her slide readers skip them; she has said so in three reviews.
- Longer method notes go to the appendix, and the "Read this first" line must point to them.
