# Chloe Martin refers to campaigns by code `CMP-yyyy-nnn`, not by title
- Campaigns are named by their code, e.g. `CMP-2026-014`, in every chart label and readout.
- Campaign titles are allowed in the appendix only.
- Reason: campaign titles were reused across quarters in 2025, and two readouts were compared as if they were the same campaign.
