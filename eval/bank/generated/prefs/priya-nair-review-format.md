# Priya Nair runs onboarding reviews as 25 minutes, one table, pre-read by 09:00
- Onboarding reviews are 25 minutes. Priya circulates a single table (step, n entering, conversion %, median minutes) by 09:00 on the day.
- No slide deck for the review; questions are taken against the table.
- Anything not on the table becomes a follow-up ticket, not a discussion item.
