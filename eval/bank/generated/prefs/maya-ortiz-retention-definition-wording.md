# Maya Ortiz describes retention as "still open at month 12, as a share of cohort book" and bans "loyalty"
- The wording used in every deck: "retention = share of the cohort book still open at month 12."
- The word "loyalty" is banned in retention text because it implies an intent the metric does not measure.
- Reason: a 2025 board paper called retention "loyalty" and the board asked for a loyalty programme that did not exist.
