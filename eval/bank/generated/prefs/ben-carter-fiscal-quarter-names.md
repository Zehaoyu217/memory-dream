# Ben Carter writes quarters as FY27 FQ1, never as FY27 Q1
- Finance quarter labels use "FQ": FY27 FQ1 is April to June 2026, FY27 FQ2 is July to September 2026.
- The plain "Q" label is reserved for calendar quarters in the data.
- Reason: a calendar-quarter "Q3" in a board paper was read as the fiscal quarter and two numbers were compared across the wrong periods.
