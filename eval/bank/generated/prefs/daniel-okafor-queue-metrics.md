# Daniel Okafor judges alert queues by precision@50 and precision@200, not AUC alone
- Alert-queue reviews report precision@50 and precision@200 on the latest fraud holdout, plus alerts per 1,000 transactions.
- AUC may appear in the model card but never leads a queue review.
- Reason: the ops team works about 50 alerts a day, so precision@50 is the number that decides staffing.
