# Maya Ortiz colours the focal retention cohort in brand blue and greys out the rest
- On retention line charts, the focal cohort (the one the slide is about) is brand blue `#1F4E9A` at full weight.
- All other cohorts are grey `#B8BEC7` at 1px.
- She does not allow rainbow palettes on retention charts; more than three coloured series means split into small multiples.
- Reason: in the Q4 2025 retention review a seven-colour chart was read as seven equal stories and the decision slide was lost.
