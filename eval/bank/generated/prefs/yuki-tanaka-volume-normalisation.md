# Yuki Tanaka normalises call volumes per 1,000 active accounts, never raw call counts
- Call volumes in CX reviews are calls per 1,000 active accounts in the month, from `callcenter.calls` and `cards.accounts_monthly`.
- Raw call counts are shown only in the appendix.
- Reason: raw counts rose 12% in 2026-05 while calls per 1,000 accounts were flat, because the book grew.
