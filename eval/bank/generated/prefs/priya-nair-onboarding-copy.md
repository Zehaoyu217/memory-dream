# Priya Nair wants onboarding copy to say "drop-off", not "churn"
- In onboarding decks and customer-facing copy, a customer who stops mid-funnel is a "drop-off".
- The word "churn" is reserved for the retention team's account-closure metric, where it has a fixed definition.
- Reason: product managers read "churn" in onboarding slides as account closure and asked for the wrong fix in 2025.
