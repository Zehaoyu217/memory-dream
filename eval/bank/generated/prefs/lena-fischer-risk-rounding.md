# Lena Fischer rounds Gini and KS to 2 decimals and exposure to $m with 1 decimal
- Risk packs show Gini and KS to 2 decimals, and PD and default rates to 2 decimals in percent (e.g. 3.84%).
- Exposure and balances are in $m with one decimal (e.g. $412.7m), never $k in the risk pack.
- Ratios such as loss-to-book are 2 decimals in percent.
