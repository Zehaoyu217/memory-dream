# Yuki Tanaka reports CSAT on a 1 to 5 scale as top-2-box %, never as a mean
- CSAT is reported as the share of responses scoring 4 or 5 (top-2-box), in percent to one decimal.
- A mean on a 1 to 5 scale is not shown, because Yuki says a 3.9 hides a bimodal split.
- Example: "CSAT top-2-box 71.3% (n = 4,812 surveys, July 2026)."
