# Daniel Okafor wants fraud alerts worded tersely: one number, then one action
- Fraud alert messages to the ops queue are two sentences at most: the number that triggered the alert, then the action.
- Example he approved: "Velocity 7 txns in 10 min on card ending 4411. Hold and call the cardholder."
- Hedging words ("might", "possibly", "seems") are struck. If the team is unsure, the alert states the confidence as a number instead.
