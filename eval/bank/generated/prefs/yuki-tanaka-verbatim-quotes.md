# Yuki Tanaka wants each CX finding backed by at most two verbatim call quotes with call IDs
- Every customer-experience finding cites up to two verbatim quotes from `callcenter.calls`, each with its call ID.
- More than two quotes is treated as cherry-picking; Yuki has cut slides for it.
- Paraphrases never go in the quote field; they go in the finding text.
