# Omar Haddad wants caveats as numbered footnotes that cite the regulatory reference
- Compliance caveats go in numbered footnotes, each citing its reference (e.g. "[3] Fair-lending review FL-2025-07, section 4.2").
- A caveat may also appear in the body, but never only in the body.
- This differs from Maya Ortiz's "Read this first" rule; compliance pages keep footnotes.
