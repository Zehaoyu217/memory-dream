# Lena Fischer wants PSI and risk-tier bar charts to start at zero
- Any bar chart of PSI by feature, or of default rate by risk tier, starts its y-axis at zero.
- She draws the 0.10 and 0.25 PSI thresholds as dashed horizontal lines, labelled "stable" and "review".
- Reason: a truncated axis made the 2025 limit-model PSI move from 0.03 to 0.13 look like a cliff rather than a step.
