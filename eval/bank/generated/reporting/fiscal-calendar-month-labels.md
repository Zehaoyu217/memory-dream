# Month labels in decks are FY27-M01 to FY27-M12, with M01 as April
- `FY27-M01` is April 2026 and `FY27-M12` is March 2027.
- Calendar month names (e.g. "Apr 2026") may appear in the body text, but charts use the FY label on the axis.
- Example: "Retention at FY27-M04 (July 2026): 91.2% of book".
