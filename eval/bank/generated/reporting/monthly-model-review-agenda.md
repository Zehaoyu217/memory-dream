# The monthly model review is 60 minutes on the third Tuesday, in a fixed agenda
- Agenda: drift (PSI on the top-20 features), performance on the latest holdout, alert or queue performance, decisions, owners.
- Each model gets 8 minutes; models with a PSI trigger get 15.
- Decisions are logged as they are made (see reporting/monthly-review-decision-log).
