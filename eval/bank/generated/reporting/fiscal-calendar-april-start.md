# The fiscal year starts on 1 April: FY27 is April 2026 to March 2027
- FY27 Q1 is April to June 2026; FY27 Q4 is January to March 2027.
- Calendar quarters are used only for the raw data, never for reporting labels in decks.
- The fiscal year is the one the finance team reports against; all board figures use it.
