# CX metrics: CSAT is top-2-box percent; call volume is calls per 1,000 active accounts
- CSAT: share of surveys scoring 4 or 5 on the 1 to 5 scale, shown to one decimal.
- Call volume: calls in `callcenter.calls` per 1,000 active accounts in `cards.accounts_monthly` for the month.
- Raw call counts are kept in the appendix only.
