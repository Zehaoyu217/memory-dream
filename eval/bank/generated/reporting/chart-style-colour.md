# Chart colour: brand blue for the primary series, grey for context, one orange highlight per chart
- Primary series are brand blue `#1F4E9A`; context series are grey `#B8BEC7`.
- Orange `#E07B24` is used for exactly one highlighted series per chart, never two.
- Charts with more than three coloured series are split into small multiples instead.
