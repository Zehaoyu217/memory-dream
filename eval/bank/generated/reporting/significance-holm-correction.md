# Multi-arm and segment tests use the Holm correction at a family-wise alpha of 0.05, for up to 10 segments
- A test family with up to 10 segments is adjusted with Holm at family-wise alpha 0.05.
- Each segment's adjusted p-value is reported in the appendix, not the body.
- Tests on more than 10 segments are not covered by this note; see the later rule in reporting/significance-bh-segments.
