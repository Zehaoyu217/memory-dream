# Currency is $m with 1 decimal in executive summaries and $k in appendices; units are never mixed on one page
- Executive summaries: $m, one decimal (e.g. $1,284.3m).
- Appendices and detail tables: $k, rounded to the nearest $1k.
- A page that shows both units is returned for correction.
