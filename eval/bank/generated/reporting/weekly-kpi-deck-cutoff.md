# Weekly KPI deck data cutoff is Sunday 23:59 UTC, with the data pull at Monday 06:00 UTC
- Every weekly figure covers the seven days ending Sunday 23:59 UTC.
- The data pull runs Monday 06:00 UTC, after the `cards.txn_daily` SLA, so the deck is built from a complete week.
- Figures pulled after 06:00 are not added to the deck; they wait for next week.
