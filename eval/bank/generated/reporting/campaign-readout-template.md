# Campaign readouts have four sections: question, lift with 95% CI, cost per response in $, recommendation
- Each readout is identified by its campaign code, e.g. `CMP-2026-014`, in the title.
- Lift is shown as a point estimate with its 95% CI, per the uplift rule.
- Cost per response is in $ and sits in the same table as the lift.
