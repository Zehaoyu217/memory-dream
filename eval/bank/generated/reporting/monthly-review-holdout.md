# Each monthly review scores the latest out-of-time holdout named `<project>-oot-2026h1`; random holdouts are excluded
- The holdout is the current half-year's out-of-time set, named `<project>-oot-2026h1` (or `2026h2` from July).
- Random holdouts are not reported in the monthly review, even as a secondary view.
- The holdout's n and date range are printed in the title of the performance slide.
