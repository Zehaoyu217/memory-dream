# Weekly decks have 8 main slides, monthly decks up to 12; appendices have no limit
- The weekly deck is 8 main slides (see reporting/weekly-kpi-deck-structure).
- The monthly model review deck is up to 12 main slides, counting the title.
- Everything else goes in the appendix, which has no slide limit.
