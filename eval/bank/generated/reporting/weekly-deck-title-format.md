# Weekly deck titles read "Weekly KPI, w/c DD Mon YYYY", where the date is the Monday of the reporting week
- The title uses the Monday of the week covered by the data pull, e.g. "Weekly KPI, w/c 05 Oct 2026".
- The data cutoff date (see reporting/weekly-kpi-cutoff-v2) is printed in the footer, not the title.
- Titles of monthly decks use the FY label, e.g. "Monthly model review, FY27-M07".
