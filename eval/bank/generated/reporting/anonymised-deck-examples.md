# Deck examples use hashed sample ids and never customer names or card numbers
- Examples in decks use `sample_id` values hashed with the team salt; no name, address or card number appears.
- A slide that shows a customer example must say "illustrative" in its subtitle.
- Verbatim call quotes follow Yuki Tanaka's rule: at most two per finding, with call IDs.
