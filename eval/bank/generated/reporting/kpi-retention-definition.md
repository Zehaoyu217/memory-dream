# Retention at month 12 is the share of the cohort's accounts still open at M+12
- Cohort = accounts opened in the same calendar month; retention at M+12 = cohort accounts open at M+12 divided by cohort accounts at M0.
- Reported as a percent of cohort book to one decimal.
- A later revision changes how charged-off accounts count; see reporting/kpi-retention-definition-v2 for the current rule.
