# Label the biggest change on the line with its date and value; use direct labels instead of legends where they fit
- The largest movement in a time series gets one annotation: the date and the value, e.g. "2026-03: 0.91".
- Legends are dropped when the series can be labelled at its line end.
- Annotations are not used for more than two events per chart.
