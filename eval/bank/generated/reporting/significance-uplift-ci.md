# Uplift in decks is a point estimate with a 95% confidence interval, not a p-value
- Example of the required form: "+1.8 pts (95% CI +0.6 to +3.0)".
- P-values stay in the analysis notebook; decks do not show them.
- An uplift whose CI includes zero is labelled "no clear difference", not "not significant".
