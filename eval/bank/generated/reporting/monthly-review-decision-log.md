# Every decision in a monthly review is logged as one line: decision, owner, date, model id
- Format: `YYYY-MM-DD | decision | owner | model id`, e.g. `2026-03-17 | retrain cli-xgb-v3 on 2026q1 | Aisha Khan | cli-xgb-v3`.
- The log lives in the review folder and is read back at the start of the next review.
- A decision not in the log did not happen for reporting purposes.
