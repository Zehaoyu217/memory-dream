# From June 2026, charged-off accounts count as not retained, and retention is measured on cohort book balances
- Retention at M+12 = cohort book balance at M+12 from accounts still open, divided by the cohort book balance at M0.
- Any account charged off within the window counts as not retained, even if it was open at M+12.
- Reason: the earlier count-based rule showed 92% retention for a cohort in which 6% had been charged off, which Ben Carter's finance team could not reconcile.
