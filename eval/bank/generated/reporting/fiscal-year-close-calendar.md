# Year-end close: March data is final on the 12th working day of April, and board packs follow in mid-April
- March 2027 (FY27-M12) data is final on the 12th working day of April 2027.
- The board pack for FY27 is circulated the following Tuesday.
- Monthly reviews for the closing month are held after the close, not before.
