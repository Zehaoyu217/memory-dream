# Rates are shown only for groups with at least 200 cases or accounts; smaller groups show "n<200"
- The minimum base for a rate shown in any deck is 200 cases or accounts.
- Groups below 200 show the count and the text "n<200" in place of the rate.
- The rule applies to segments, campaigns and collections buckets alike.
