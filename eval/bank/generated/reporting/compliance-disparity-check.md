# The fair-lending check computes the approval-rate ratio per group and flags anything below 0.80
- Ratio = lowest group approval rate divided by the highest, on the deck's population and date window.
- A ratio below 0.80 is flagged and footnoted; Omar Haddad reviews the flag before publication.
- The check is run by the analyst on the deck's own snapshot, not a separate extract.
