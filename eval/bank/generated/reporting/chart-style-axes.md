# Bars start at zero; line charts may use a non-zero baseline when the axis says so; no dual axes
- Every bar chart starts its value axis at zero.
- Line charts may use a non-zero baseline, but the axis must be labelled with the baseline value.
- Dual-axis charts are not used; two measures go in two panels sharing an x-axis.
