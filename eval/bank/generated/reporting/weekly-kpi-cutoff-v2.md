# From 2026-03-02 the weekly KPI cutoff is Saturday 23:59 UTC, not Sunday
- Every weekly figure now covers the seven days ending Saturday 23:59 UTC; Sunday's data is excluded from the deck.
- The pull stays at Monday 06:00 UTC.
- Reason: the Sunday batch for `cards.txn_daily` was landing after 06:00 on four Mondays in 2026-01 and February, so the deck showed partial Sundays.
- Sunday activity is reported in the following week's deck as a separate line.
