# Percentages are shown to 1 decimal; risk-table rates and ratios to 2 decimals
- Retention, share and campaign-response percentages: 1 decimal, e.g. 88.4%.
- Default rates, PD and roll rates in risk tables: 2 decimals, e.g. 3.84%.
- Changes in percentages are written as "pts" (percentage points), e.g. "+1.8 pts", never "%".
