# In the KPI deck, book is open accounts at month end, excluding charged-off accounts, shown as count and $ balance
- Book = accounts open at the last day of the month, excluding charged-off accounts.
- Book is reported as a count and as a $ balance; the $ balance is the one used for "% of book" in finance text.
- Source: `cards.accounts_monthly`, month-end snapshot.
