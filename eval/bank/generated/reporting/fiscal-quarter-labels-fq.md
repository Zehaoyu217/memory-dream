# Board packs label fiscal quarters FQ, e.g. FY27 FQ1, and reserve Q for calendar quarters
- Finance quarter labels in board packs are `FY27 FQ1` to `FY27 FQ4`.
- `Q1` to `Q4` are used only for calendar quarters in data tables.
- The same rule applies in decks; see prefs/ben-carter-fiscal-quarter-names for the reason.
