# A charge-off haircut is required: the interest model overstates by $4.2M a quarter without it
The 2025-Q1-only check in fnd-rev26-no-haircut missed the promo cohorts. Over four quarters the no-haircut error is $4.2M a quarter (exp-302).
With the haircut the error is $0.9M a quarter (exp-303).
rev26-interest-v2 is the model of record from this date.
