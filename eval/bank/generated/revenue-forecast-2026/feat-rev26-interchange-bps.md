# feat-rev26-interchange-bps: blended interchange rate in basis points by MCC group
Blended interchange rate in basis points by MCC group (groceries, travel, dining, fuel, other), from the ledger divided by volume.
Flat-bps baseline has 4.8% MAPE on 2024-H2; the MCC-group version has 2.1% (exp-301).
Used by rev26-interchange-v1.
