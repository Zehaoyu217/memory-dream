# Monthly interest accrual by segment, 2024-01 to 2026-03
Monthly interest income accrual by segment, built from `cards.accounts_monthly` balances times APR, reconciled to the finance interest ledger.
Original build 18 Mar 2025 covered 2024-01 to 2025-02; extended to 2026-03 on 8 Apr 2026.
Accrual is gross of charge-offs; the haircut is applied in the model (feat-rev26-charge-off-haircut).
