# About 70% of interchange forecast variance comes from volume, 30% from the bps mix
Decomposing the interchange forecast error on 2025-H2: volume explains about 70%, the MCC-group bps mix about 30%.
The volume driver is therefore the one to watch; hence the reconciled spend forecast (exp-305).
The bps mix changes slowly and is reviewed each quarter.
