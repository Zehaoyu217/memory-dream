# Q: Can we use the bureau score dated in the observation month? A: No, it has to be at least 30 days before observation.
A same-month pull raised AUC on the 2025 default model by 0.02 on the holdout, which is the signature of leakage. Lena Fischer rejected the feature.
The 30-day lag rule was applied from the 2025-05 rerun onwards.
