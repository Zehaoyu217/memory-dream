# Q: What cutoff does the collections model use for its call queue? A: The validation-slice cutoff from the cost matrix, not 0.5.
Costs are $95 per missed roll and $22 per collections call. The OOT flagged rate has to stay within 10% of the validation flagged rate.
Raj Patel's team confirmed the queue capacity, and the cutoff is re-set on validation each quarter.
