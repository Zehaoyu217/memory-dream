# Q: Why did the 60-day roll rate fall by about 3 points in the 2025 H1 reanalysis? A: Dropping cases that closed in full had inflated it; censoring them at close corrects it.
Raj Patel's team reran the analysis with closed-in-full cases censored at close date. The roll rate per 100 open cases is the figure reported.
