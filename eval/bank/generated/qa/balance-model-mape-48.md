# Q: Why is the balance model MAPE 48 percent in the monthly review? A: Near-zero balances dominate MAPE, so report WAPE instead.
The August 2025 run showed 48% MAPE, driven by accounts under $50. WAPE on the same run is 12%, and that is the figure in the deck.
Ben Carter's team agreed to headline WAPE from the October review.
