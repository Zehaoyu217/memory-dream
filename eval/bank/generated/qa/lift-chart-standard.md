# Q: What does a lift curve in the deck plot on each axis? A: Cumulative lift on the y-axis against decile on the x-axis, with a dashed line at 1.0.
Decile counts sit under the x-axis and the top-decile value is labelled directly. Chloe Martin's deck template uses this standard.
