# Q: How wide is the uplift@20% interval for the Q3 2025 offer test? A: About 2.0 points, with a 95% interval of 1.1 to 2.9 percentage points.
The interval comes from 500 account-level resamples within each arm, using the percentile method. Chloe Martin's team recorded it in the offer test readout.
