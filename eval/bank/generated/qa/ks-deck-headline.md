# Q: Why is KS the headline in the monthly review rather than AUC? A: KS is read at the operating point, while AUC only summarises ranking.
After the May 2025 review, where AUC rose but KS fell at the cutoff, Alex Chen agreed to headline KS. Gini stays in the table beside it.
