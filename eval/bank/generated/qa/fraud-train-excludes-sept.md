# Q: Why is 2025-09 excluded from the fraud training set? A: Late-September labels had not matured at the 2026-01-15 extract.
Under the 120-day maturity rule, late-September transactions were not yet 120 days old at the extract, so Daniel Okafor's team dropped that month.
Training uses observation months at least four months back.
