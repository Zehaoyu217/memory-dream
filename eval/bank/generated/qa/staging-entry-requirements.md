# Q: What must a model have before it moves to staging? A: A snapshot id, OOT metrics, a PSI report and a model card, with Aisha Khan's sign-off.
The retention model cleared all four on 2025-11-05. Staging also needs two weeks of shadow scoring, which started on 2025-11-06.
