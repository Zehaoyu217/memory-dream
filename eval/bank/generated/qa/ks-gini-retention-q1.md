# Q: What KS and Gini did the retention model show in the Q1 2026 review? A: KS 0.31 and Gini 0.52 on n = 48,200 OOT accounts.
Gini is derived as 2 times AUC minus 1, which implies an AUC of about 0.76. The deck headline is KS, read at the operating point.
