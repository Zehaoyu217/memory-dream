# Q: Which observation months form the OOT set for the retention propensity model? A: Observation 2025-07 to 2025-09, with training ending 2024-12.
The six months from 2025-01 to 2025-06 are the gap and are not used. Split is on observation_date, so no account appears in both training and OOT.
