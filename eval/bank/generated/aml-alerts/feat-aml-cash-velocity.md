# feat-aml-cash-velocity: 30-day cash total against the prior 90-day average
Ratio of the card's cash total over the last 30 days to its 90-day average, per 30-day period.
Helps separate sudden cash use from steady use; shipped with round-amount in aml-xgb-v2.
