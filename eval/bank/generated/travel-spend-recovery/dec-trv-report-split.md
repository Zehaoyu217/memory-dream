# Decision: report domestic and cross-border travel spend separately, not as one blended series
The blended series hid a cross-border lag through 2025 (fnd-trv-plateau) and then its recovery.
From the June 2026 pack, domestic and cross-border are reported as separate lines with their own run-rates.
The blend is kept as a footnote only.
