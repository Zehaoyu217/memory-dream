# Hotel spend trails airline spend by about one quarter in the recovery
In the 2024-Q4 cohort, hotel spend returns about one quarter after airline spend (exp-507).
The lag holds across tenure bands; it is a timing effect, not a different group of cardholders.
Hotel forecasts therefore use airline spend lagged one quarter as a regressor in the next model round.
