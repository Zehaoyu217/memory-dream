# feat-pa-promo-exit-cliff: balance at promo expiry relative to the balance at month 1
Ratio of the balance in the month of expiry to the balance in month 1, from the balance panel.
Accounts with a ratio below 0.5 are the cliff group; churn is concentrated there. Used in pa-churn-hazard-v1.
