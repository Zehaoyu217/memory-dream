# Decision: the default promo is 6 months at 0%; 12 months only for scores 640-699
The 6-month arm keeps 69% of the BT lift at lower exposure. The 12-month promo is kept only in the 640-699 band, where response is highest and margin holds.
Score 700 and over get the 6-month promo. The 12-month variant is a pilot for that band only.
