# `promo.ab_assignments` randomizes 96,000 accounts into three arms, Feb to Apr 2026
Random assignment at account level: control 32,000 (19.99%), arm B 32,000 (0% for 12 months), arm C 32,000 (0% for 6 months). Promo start is the assignment month.
Eligibility: standard-APR cardholders with no promo in the last 12 months and a bureau score on file.
