# Finding: the 640-699 band is where the 12-month promo both responds and pays
exp-507: strongest BT response in the 640-699 band. Under 640 the response is similar but the margin is negative; at 700 and above the response is weak.
So the 12-month promo has a narrow sweet spot, not a general case. Feeds dec-pa-no-12m-subprime and dec-pa-6m-default.
