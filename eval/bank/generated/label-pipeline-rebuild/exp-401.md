# exp-401: churn rate on 2025-H1 snapshots is 7.9 percent under the 60-day rule and 6.1 percent under 90 days
Computed both windows on the 4.6M account-snapshots from 2025-H1, same accounts, same snapshot dates.
60-day rule: 7.9 percent churn. 90-day rule: 6.1 percent. The difference is accounts that were quiet for 60 to 90 days and then transacted again.
