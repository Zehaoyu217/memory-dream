# Fraud labels need 120 days of maturity before a model is evaluated on them
exp-402: median label lag 71 days, 95th percentile 128 days. An evaluation at 90 days would undercount fraud by a large share.
Any fraud holdout must use transactions at least 120 days old. Applies to every fraud evaluation from here on.
