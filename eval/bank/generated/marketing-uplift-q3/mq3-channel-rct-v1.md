# mq3-channel-rct-v1: incremental spend per dollar of contact cost, by channel, from the Q2 RCT
Channel-cell means within treatment, compared with control on 14-day spend, divided by channel cost from `mkt.channel_costs`.
In-app 14.2, email 9.4, push 6.1, direct mail 3.1 incremental dollars per dollar of cost (exp-304).
Channel was randomized within treatment, so the channel effects are causal; each channel was one touch per account per cell.
