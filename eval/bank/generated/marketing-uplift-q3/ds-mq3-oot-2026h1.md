# `mq3-oot-2026h1` is the Q1 2026 RCT: 240,000 contacts, 10% control, used as the out-of-time set
Q1 2026 campaign (Jan 12 to Mar 15 2026), randomized the same way as Q2. Used only as OOT: models trained on Q2 never see it.
Control 24,000 accounts; 14-day response base rate 1.9%.
About half of its app sessions fall after the February redesign, so its app features use the mapped counts.
