# Finding: a response model targets people who would buy anyway; about 60% of top-20% responders are non-incremental
Among the top-20% response scores in the Q2 split, roughly 60% of responders would have bought without the contact (control-arm rate for similar scores).
Response rate is the wrong target for a spend campaign: $0.90 incremental per contact for response-only, against $2.40 for uplift.
Feeds dec-mq3-target-uplift.
