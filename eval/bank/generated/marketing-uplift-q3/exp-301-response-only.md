# exp-301: response-only targeting puts 3.8% responders in the top 20%, but only $0.90 incremental spend per contact
mq3-response-gbm-v1 on the 30% split of the Q2 RCT (n=330,000). Top 20% by response score: response 3.8% against a 2.1% base.
Incremental 14-day spend per contact in that top 20% is $0.90, against $2.40 for the uplift model (exp-302).
Many responders in the top 20% would have bought without the contact.
