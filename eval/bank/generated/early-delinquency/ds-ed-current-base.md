# Current-account base: accounts at 0 DPD from cards.accounts_monthly, the population the early-delinquency model scores
Monthly snapshot of accounts at 0 DPD from `cards.accounts_monthly`. About 2.1M accounts per snapshot.
Excluded: charged-off, frozen, and accounts under 3 months on book.
Grain is account-month. Snapshot is taken on the second business day, so month-end payments are already posted.
