# active account is a purchase or a payment in 90 days (from 2026-03-01)
The old note counted purchases only. From 2026-03-01 an open account is active if it has a purchase or a payment in the last 90 days. This was a product decision by Maya Ortiz for retention reporting.
Affected: active counts rise about 6%. Dec 2024 active accounts restated from 1.19m to 1.27m on the new rule.
