# the 180 DPD charge-off rule starts 2026-03-01
This corrects charge-off-180dpd. The 180 DPD rule took effect on 2026-03-01, not 2026-01-01. Accounts charged off in Jan-Feb 2026 still used 120 DPD because the loss-ledger switch was delayed.
Affected: Jan-Feb 2026 charge-off counts are on the 120 DPD basis and must not be compared with later months directly.
