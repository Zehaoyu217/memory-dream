# FPR divides by legitimate transactions
The old note divided flags by all transactions. FPR is flagged legitimate transactions divided by all legitimate transactions.
Affected: Sept 2024 FPR restated from 0.49% to 0.53% (14,200 flags over 2.68m legitimate transactions).
