# churn includes 6-month dormancy
The old note defined churn as closure only. The churn label is now closure OR no purchase and no payment for 6 months. `cards.churn_labels` was rebuilt on 2026-03-16 on this basis.
Affected: Sept 2024 monthly churn restated from 1.1% to 3.4%.
