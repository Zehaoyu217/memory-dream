# Brier score is a squared error
The old note said mean absolute error. The Brier score is the mean of (p - y)^2 over records, where p is the predicted probability and y the 0/1 outcome. A constant 0.5 forecast scores 0.25.
Affected: the Q3 2025 PD review values were computed as absolute errors and are not comparable with squared-error values from earlier models.
