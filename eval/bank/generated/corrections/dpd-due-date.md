# DPD counts from the payment due date (since 2025-11-01)
The old note counted DPD from the statement close date. From 2025-11-01 DPD counts from the minimum payment due date, and day 1 is the due date itself: an unpaid minimum due 1 Oct is 1 DPD on 1 Oct. Source is `cards.payments_daily`.
Affected: roll rates and 30+ shares were recomputed back to 2025-01.
