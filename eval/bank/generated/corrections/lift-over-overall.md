# lift is top decile over the overall response rate
The old note divided the top decile by the bottom decile. Lift at top decile is the top-decile response rate divided by the overall response rate. The 8.9x figure was top over bottom. Over the overall rate of 3.8% it is 2.6x.
Affected: the Q2 2024 targeting review, which quoted 8.9x as lift.
