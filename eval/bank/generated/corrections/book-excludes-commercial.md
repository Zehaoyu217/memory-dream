# book excludes commercial cards from 2026-01-01
The old note said the book includes commercial cards. From 2026-01-01 the book is receivables on open consumer accounts only, excluding commercial cards and charged-off balances. Ben Carter's finance pack reports commercial separately.
Affected: the % of book denominators in the weekly KPI deck. Q4 2024 prime share restated from 63% to 68% on the consumer base ($3.8bn).
