# response rate divides by contacts, not by the book
The old note divided responses by accounts in the book. Response rate is responses divided by accounts actually contacted. The Sept 2024 CLI email was 5.2% over contacts; the 3.4% figure was over book.
Affected: campaign decks from 2024 Q3 onward, using `marketing.campaign_responses`.
