# vintage is origination month, not limit band
The old note grouped vintages by limit band. A vintage is the cohort of accounts opened in the same month; limit band is a segment, not a vintage. Vintage curves plot cumulative default by months on book, one curve per origination month.
Affected: the Q2 2025 review vintage curves were redrawn by origination month.
