# minimum payment is 1% plus interest and fees, $25 floor
The old note gave 2% of the balance with a $25 floor. From 2026-01-01 the minimum is 1% of the balance plus interest and fees, with a $25 floor, or the full balance if lower.
Affected: a $4,000 balance with $36 of interest now shows a $76 minimum, not $80.
