# contact rate is calls per 100 accounts per month
The old note gave calls per account per year. Contact rate is calls per 100 accounts per month, from `callcenter.calls`.
Affected: the old 0.4 per year equals 3.3 per 100 per month. Yuki Tanaka's CX dashboard was relabelled to match.
