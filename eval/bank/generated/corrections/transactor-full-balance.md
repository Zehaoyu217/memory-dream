# transactor pays the full statement balance
The old note said a transactor pays at least the minimum. A transactor pays the full statement balance each cycle and pays no interest. Paying only the minimum makes a cardholder a revolver.
Affected: offer targeting excluded minimum-payers as transactors. About 9% of active accounts were re-tagged.
