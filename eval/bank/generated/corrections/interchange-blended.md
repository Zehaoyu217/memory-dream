# interchange: blended rate is 1.9%, not 2.5%
The old note gave 2.5% on credit purchases. The blended rate on our book is 1.9%. 2.5% was the premium-card rate only; standard cards sit around 1.6-1.8%. Example: $100 of purchases at 1.9% earns $1.90.
Affected: the Q4 2025 finance pack used 2.5% for interchange revenue and was restated to 1.9% blended.
