# precision@k divides by k; campaigns report at k = 20%
The old note used total positives as the denominator at k = 10%. precision@k is positives in the top-k divided by k. From 2026-01 campaign targeting reports precision and uplift at k = 20% (uplift@20%).
Affected: KPI deck columns were renamed. The 10% columns are kept for retention models only.
