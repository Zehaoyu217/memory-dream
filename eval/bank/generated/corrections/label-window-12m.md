# default label window is 12 months (from 2025-09-01)
The old note gave 6 months for default. From 2025-09-01 the default (PD) label is 90+ DPD within 12 months of observation, after Lena Fischer's credit-risk team moved the PD horizon to 12 months. The churn label stays at 6 months in `cards.churn_labels`.
Affected: PD models from v2 onward. v1 numbers are on the 6-month label and are not comparable.
