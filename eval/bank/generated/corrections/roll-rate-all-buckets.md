# roll rate covers every bucket and is balance-weighted
The old note gave only 0-to-30 by account count. Roll rates are computed for every transition (0-to-30, 30-to-60, 60-to-90 and so on) and are balance-weighted. The 0-to-30 headline stays on account count.
Affected: the Nov 2025 dashboard shows the full matrix. Sept 2024 0-to-30 stays 1.8% by count; the balance-weighted figure is 2.3%.
