# chargeback means a dispute resolved for the cardholder
The old note counted any dispute opened. A chargeback is counted only when the dispute is resolved in the cardholder's favour and the merchant settlement is reversed. Open cases stay as disputes in `cards.disputes`.
Affected: the week of 2024-09-08 showed 4,812 disputes. Only 1,930 of them were chargebacks.
