# champion-challenger needs 8 weeks plus KS and fairness checks
The old note said two weeks and a higher AUC. A challenger runs for at least 8 weeks and must pass KS and the fairness check run by Omar Haddad's compliance team before promotion.
Affected: the score-gbm-v5 challenger showed +0.01 AUC after two weeks in Sept 2024 and was not promoted.
