# LGD is measured 12 months after charge-off, net of costs
The old note measured LGD at charge-off. LGD = 1 - (recoveries within 12 months of charge-off, net of collection costs) / balance at charge-off. Source is `collections.cases`.
Affected: the $1,000 example. $280 recovered at charge-off gives 72%. With $190 recovered by month 12 and no costs, it gives 81%.
