# observation date is the last day of the statement month
The old note said the observation date is the first day of the month. It is the last day of the statement month: features use data through that day inclusive, and the label window starts the day after.
Affected: earlier monthly runs used the 1st and dropped the final month of data. The 2025 scores were rerun with the last-day observation date on 2025-04-02.
