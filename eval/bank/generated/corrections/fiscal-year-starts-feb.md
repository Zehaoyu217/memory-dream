# fiscal year starts 1 February
The old note said the fiscal year starts 1 January. From FY2026 the fiscal year starts 1 February, per Ben Carter's finance calendar. Q1 = Feb-Apr, Q2 = May-Jul, Q3 = Aug-Oct, Q4 = Nov-Jan.
Affected: quarter labels in the monthly model review. The glossary example "FY2024 Q3 = Jul-Sep 2024" is on the old calendar.
