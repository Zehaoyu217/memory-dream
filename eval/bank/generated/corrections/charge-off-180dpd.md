# charge-off happens at 180 DPD (from 2026-01-01)
The old note gave 120 DPD. From 2026-01-01 the policy is 180 DPD under Omar Haddad's compliance rules. The charge-off date is the day the account reaches 180 DPD.
Affected: the gross charge-off forecast for 2026 and any comparison with 2025 charge-off history, which is on the 120 basis.
