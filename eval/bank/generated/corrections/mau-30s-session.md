# MAU needs a session of 30 seconds or more
The old note counted any login in 30 days. MAU is distinct user_ids with a session of at least 30 seconds in the last 30 days. Bounced logins are excluded. Source is `app.sessions_daily`.
Affected: Sept 2024 MAU restated from 1.9m to 1.8m.
