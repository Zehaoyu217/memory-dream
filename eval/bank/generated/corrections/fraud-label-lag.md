# fraud labels lag by up to 60 days
The old note said the label is final on the transaction date. Confirmed fraud labels arrive up to 60 days after the transaction. Train and score with labels as they stood at observation date + 60 days, never later.
Affected: the 0.11% label rate in the Sept 2024 extract became 0.19% once the late labels arrived.
