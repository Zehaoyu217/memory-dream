# net charge-off subtracts recoveries
The old note said net charge-off equals gross. Net charge-off = gross charge-off minus recoveries credited to the loss ledger in the period. Example: Q3 2024 $38m gross less $6m recoveries gives $32m net.
Affected: finance-reported net charge-off figures for 2024 quarters.
