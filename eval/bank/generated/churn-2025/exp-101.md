# exp-101: label window sweep on churn-lr-base-2025, 60 days chosen over 90
- Same LR baseline, labels at 30, 60 and 90 days, scored on c25-oot-2024h1.
- AUC: 30d 0.661, 60d 0.693, 90d 0.704.
- Base rates: 30d 2.1%, 60d 4.3%, 90d 6.8%.
- 90d adds 0.011 AUC over 60d but its labels take 30 more days to mature (fnd-c25-90d-feedback-lag).
