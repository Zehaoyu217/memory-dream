## Q1
feat-rs-region-rate is a target-encoded, out-of-fold recovery rate by postcode region, computed on training data only and smoothed toward the book mean. It comes from the `cards.accounts_monthly` postcode joined to ds-rs-recovery-labels. In exp-303, adding it lifted Gini from 0.41 to 0.44 (KS 0.32) on recovery-scoring-holdout-2023h2 (n=6,120). exp-304 then showed a 2.9x spread in predicted recovery across region deciles at equal balance and payment history, which is the pattern compliance (Omar Haddad) flagged as a proxy for neighbourhood demographics, so on 2025-05-22 Lena Fischer and Omar Haddad decided to drop it from all recovery-scoring models, accepting the 0.02 Gini cost (0.44 to 0.42). It was removed from rs-xgb-v2, which still reached Gini 0.47 without it, and excluded from rs-xgb-v3.

## Q2
exp-403 found that a call in the prior 90 days looks like +1.8 points of open rate in a naive comparison (4.9% with a call against 3.1% without). After matching on propensity score and tenure, the effect shrank to +0.6 points (CI -0.1 to +1.3, not significant), so most of the raw gap is who calls, not what the call does (find-xs-call-overstated). On that basis call-centre outreach is not a program at scale. The follow-up causal forest (xs-call-uplift-v1, exp-406) found +1.2 points (CI 0.4–2.0) only in deciles 1–2 of xs-gbm-v2, and the team decided to send call-centre savings outreach only to deciles 1–2 and stop calls to the top deciles.

## Q3
sfc-hier-v3 builds on sfc-lgbm-v2, whose six per-segment LightGBM base forecasts it reconciles with MinT across segments and the total. It also depends on exp-207 (MinT takes total MAPE from 2.9% to 2.3% and Nov–Dec bias from -3.1% to -0.4%), fnd-sfc-q4-reconciled, and the new feat-sfc-active-accounts regressor (from ds-sfc-segment-monthly / `cards.accounts_monthly`). Its inherited inputs include feat-sfc-lags-v1 (lags 1/3/12) and feat-sfc-cpi-lag6, and sfc-lgbm-v2 also used feat-sfc-season-v2. feat-sfc-util-v1 applies to the Rewards segment only. Result: total MAPE 2.3%, segment MAPE 4.9% and Q4 bias -0.4% on sfc-oot-2025h2. Downstream, revenue-forecast-2026 uses it as its spend-volume input for interchange.

## Q7
feat-txc-normalise-v2 cleans each descriptor before tokenising: it strips store numbers, '#' codes, trailing city and state suffixes, and repeated whitespace. These appear in about 38% of raw descriptors in the 2.1M-row ds-txc-descriptor-sample. In exp-104 it lifted txc-gbm-v2 macro-F1 from 0.78 to 0.81 on holdout-2025q2 (n=2,000), mainly in Travel (+0.06) and Fuel (+0.05), with no change in training time. Its rule list is versioned in the repo and changed by pull request, and the v2 token set (char 2–5 grams) is computed on its output. It is part of the shipped txc-gbm-v3, which scores macro-F1 0.83 on txc-oot-2026h1.

## Q8
exp-205 added the payment-to-minimum ratio (feat-ed-minpay-v2) to ed-gbm-v1, without the leaky limit-change feature. AUC rose from 0.781 to 0.803 (Gini 0.606, KS 0.44) on early-delinquency-holdout-2024q4 (n=402,000). find-ed-minpay concluded the +0.022 AUC is a real, leakage-free signal and recommended it to replace the limit-change feature. That fed into ed-gbm-v2 (2025-09-10), and the feature carries on in the current ed-gbm-v3.

## Q9
fsm-churn-xgb-v2 reads 41 features from the store: v1's 40 features (originally from `ml.legacy_feats_v1`), with payment lag v2 (UTC timestamps) and the util ratio now taken from the store, plus the new feat-fsm-spend-cv-30d.
