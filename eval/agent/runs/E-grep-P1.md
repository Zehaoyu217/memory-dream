## Q1
feat-rs-region-rate is the out-of-fold, target-encoded recovery rate by postcode region. It is smoothed toward the book mean, computed on training data only, and built from the `cards.accounts_monthly` postcode joined to the recovery labels. In exp-303 it raised Gini from 0.41 to 0.44 (KS 0.32) on recovery-scoring-holdout-2023h2 (n=6,120), but exp-304 found a 2.9x spread in predicted recovery across region deciles at equal balance and payment history: the proxy for protected characteristics that Omar Haddad (compliance) had flagged, with removal costing 0.02 Gini (0.44 to 0.42). On 2025-05-22 Lena Fischer and Omar Haddad dropped it from all recovery-scoring models and accepted the 0.02 cost: it was taken out of the rs-xgb-v2 build (which still reached Gini 0.47 using post-charge-off bureau and contact features) and left out of rs-xgb-v3.

## Q2
exp-403 showed that the naive call effect was mostly selection. The open rate was 4.9% with a call in the 90 days before the offer against 3.1% without (+1.8 points), but only +0.6 points (CI -0.1 to +1.3, not significant) after matching on propensity score and tenure. This became find-xs-call-overstated (call-centre outreach is not justified as a program at scale) and led to the causal forest xs-call-uplift-v1, which found +1.2 points (CI 0.4-2.0) only in deciles 1-2 of xs-gbm-v2 and about zero in the top deciles (+0.1 in exp-406). The end result was dec-xs-call-low-deciles (2025-11-10): call-centre savings outreach goes only to deciles 1-2, and calls to the top deciles stop for this offer.

## Q3
sfc-hier-v3 builds on four notes. It takes its six per-segment LightGBM base forecasts from sfc-lgbm-v2, which it supersedes. It uses the MinT reconciliation across segments and the total from exp-207 and fnd-sfc-q4-reconciled, and it adds feat-sfc-active-accounts (active accounts per segment, from `cards.accounts_monthly`) as a regressor. Other stated inputs are feat-sfc-lags-v1 (lags 1, 3 and 12 months, "still an input") and feat-sfc-cpi-lag6 (year-on-year CPI at a six-month lag, from ds-sfc-macro-series), on ds-sfc-segment-monthly data; feat-sfc-util-v1 is Rewards-only and not used for the other five segments. On sfc-oot-2025h2 it reaches total MAPE 2.3%, segment MAPE 4.9% and Q4 bias -0.4% (sfc-lgbm-v2: 2.9%, 5.1% and -3.1%), and revenue-forecast-2026 relies on it for spend volume.

## Q7
feat-txc-normalise-v2 is a rule-based cleaning step that removes store numbers, '#' codes, trailing city and state suffixes and repeated whitespace from descriptors before tokenising. The rule list is versioned in the repo and new suffixes are added by pull request. In the 2.1M-descriptor sample from `cards.txn_daily`, about 38% of rows contain this noise. In exp-104 it raised txc-gbm-v2 macro-F1 on holdout-2025q2 (n=2,000) from 0.78 to 0.81 (Travel +0.06, Fuel +0.05, accuracy 0.87, no extra training time), and it now feeds feat-txc-descriptor-tokens-v2 and the current txc-gbm-v3 (macro-F1 0.83 on txc-oot-2026h1, n=3,100), which went into the nightly insights run on 2026-03-10.
