## Q1
feat-rs-region-rate is an out-of-fold, target-encoded recovery rate by postcode region, smoothed toward the book mean and computed on training data only. In exp-303 it lifted Gini from 0.41 to 0.44 on recovery-scoring-holdout-2023h2 (n=6,120). Omar Haddad (compliance) flagged it as a proxy for protected characteristics, and exp-304 found a 2.9x spread in predicted recovery across region deciles at equal balance and payment history. On 2025-05-22 Lena Fischer and Omar Haddad decided to drop it from all recovery-scoring models (removed from rs-xgb-v2, excluded from rs-xgb-v3) and accepted the 0.02 Gini cost (0.44 to 0.42).

## Q2
In the naive comparison, a call in the prior 90 days went with a +1.8-point higher savings open rate (4.9% vs 3.1%). After matching on propensity score and tenure the effect fell to +0.6 points (CI -0.1 to +1.3), which is not significant. The finding (find-xs-call-overstated) was that most of the gap is selection, so call outreach did not justify a program at scale. That led to the causal-forest model xs-call-uplift-v1, which found +1.2 points (CI 0.4–2.0) only in deciles 1–2 of xs-gbm-v2. As a result, outreach is now limited to deciles 1–2 and calls to the top deciles stopped (dec-xs-call-low-deciles).

## Q3
sfc-hier-v3 is built on the sfc-lgbm-v2 per-segment LightGBM base forecasts for six segments, which use feat-sfc-lags-v1 (lags 1/3/12), feat-sfc-season-v2 (Fourier terms plus retail-calendar-v3 events) and feat-sfc-cpi-lag6. On top of that it adds feat-sfc-active-accounts as a regressor and uses MinT reconciliation across the segments and the total (exp-207, fnd-sfc-q4-reconciled). Underneath sit ds-sfc-segment-monthly (cards.accounts_monthly + crm.contacts), the holiday calendar and the macro CPI series. feat-sfc-util-v1 is used only in the Rewards segment model. Result: total MAPE 2.3% (vs 2.9%), segment MAPE 4.9% and Q4 bias -0.4% on sfc-oot-2025h2; revenue-forecast-2026 uses it downstream.

## Q6
app-scorecard-v3's Gini fell from 0.41 (holdout-2025q1) to 0.33 on holdout-2026q1 (n=18,900), mostly because the v8 bureau score drifted. Lena Fischer then set a target of OOT Gini 0.42 with no manual overrides, and approved the rebuild on 2026-06-26. The rebuild keeps the explainable points-based logistic form, since the GBMs failed the reason-code/adverse-action test. It adds v10 bureau bands, reject-inference weights from app-gbm-v2 and the income estimate (thin-file approval 19% to 23%). v4 reached Gini 0.42 vs 0.35 for v3 on application-scorecard-oot-2026h1 (n=61,700).

## Q7
feat-txc-normalise-v2 cleans the raw descriptor before tokenising: it strips store numbers, '#' codes, trailing city/state suffixes and repeated whitespace (these appear in about 38% of raw descriptors). The rule list is versioned in the repo and changed by pull request. In exp-104 it lifted txc-gbm-v2 macro-F1 from 0.78 to 0.81 on holdout-2025q2 (n=2,000), mostly in Travel (+0.06) and Fuel (+0.05), with no change in training time. It feeds feat-txc-descriptor-tokens-v2 and the current txc-gbm-v3 (macro-F1 0.83 on txc-oot-2026h1), and the char-CNN that was not shipped also ran on normalised descriptors.

## Q8
In exp-205, adding feat-ed-minpay-v2 (the payment-to-minimum ratio) to ed-gbm-v1 without the limit-change feature lifted AUC from 0.781 to 0.803 (Gini 0.606, KS 0.44) on early-delinquency-holdout-2024q4 (n=402,000). find-ed-minpay concluded it is the real second signal (+0.022 AUC) with no leakage, because it uses posting dates, and recommended it as the replacement for the leaky limit-change feature. It went into ed-gbm-v2 (2025-09-10) and is still in the current ed-gbm-v3.

## Q9
fsm-churn-xgb-v2 reads 41 feature-store features. These are v1's 40 features (originally from ml.legacy_feats_v1), with payment lag and util ratio taken from the store, plus the new store-only feat-fsm-spend-cv-30d. It reads feat-fsm-payment-lag-v2 only, which uses UTC timestamps (legacy mismatch 0.2% vs 3.1% for v1). It also reads feat-fsm-util-ratio under the store's latest-effective-limit month-end rule. That rule was accepted at parity review, where 211 of 214 features passed. Holdout-2025q2 AUC is 0.791 vs 0.782 for v1, and it moved to production on store features on 2026-03-02.
