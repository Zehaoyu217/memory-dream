## Q1
feat-rs-region-rate is a target-encoded recovery rate by postcode region. It is computed out-of-fold on training data only, smoothed toward the book mean, and built from the `cards.accounts_monthly` postcode joined to the recovery labels. In exp-303 it lifted rs-xgb-v1's Gini from 0.41 to 0.44 (KS 0.32) on recovery-scoring-holdout-2023h2 (n=6,120). However, exp-304 showed a 2.9x spread in predicted recovery across region deciles at equal balance and payment history, which is the proxy pattern for neighbourhood demographics that Omar Haddad (compliance) had flagged; removing it cost only 0.02 Gini (0.44 to 0.42). On 2025-05-22 Lena Fischer and Omar Haddad decided to drop it from all recovery-scoring models and accept that cost: it was removed from the rs-xgb-v2 build and excluded from rs-xgb-v3, and rs-xgb-v2 reached Gini 0.47 without it by using post-charge-off bureau and contact features (exp-306).

## Q2
The naive comparison on the offer log showed a savings open rate of 4.9% for cardholders who called in the prior 90 days against 3.1% for those who did not (+1.8 points). ## Q3
sfc-hier-v3 is the current segment spend forecast: total MAPE 2.3% and segment MAPE 4.9% on sfc-oot-2025h2, against 2.9% and 5.1% for sfc-lgbm-v2. It is built from LightGBM base forecasts for the six segments, carried over from sfc-lgbm-v2 (which it supersedes), reconciled with MinT across the segments and the total (exp-207). Its inputs are feat-sfc-lags-v1 (log spend at lags 1, 3 and 12), feat-sfc-cpi-lag6 (year-on-year CPI lagged six months) and a new regressor, feat-sfc-active-accounts, all built from ds-sfc-segment-monthly (`cards.accounts_monthly` + `crm.contacts`); its adoption also rests on fnd-sfc-q4-reconciled (Q4 bias -0.4%). Dependencies also run the other way: revenue-forecast-2026 (ds-rev26-spend-volume, rev26-interchange-v1, rev26-combined-v1) uses it as the spend-volume input for interchange.

## Q7
feat-txc-normalise-v2 (2025-09-22) is a rule-based cleaner that runs on descriptors before tokenising. It removes store numbers, '#' hash codes, trailing city and state suffixes, and repeated whitespace, and was built from ds-txc-descriptor-sample (2.1M descriptors from `cards.txn_daily`, Jan-Jun 2025); its rule list is versioned in the repo, and new suffixes are added by pull request, not by the model. In exp-104 it lifted txc-gbm-v2's macro-F1 from 0.78 to 0.81 on holdout-2025q2 (n=2,000), mostly in Travel and Fuel. It is still in use: the v2 tokens (feat-txc-descriptor-tokens-v2) are computed after it, and both feed txc-gbm-v3, shipped on 2026-03-10 (macro-F1 0.83 on txc-oot-2026h1); it also fed the char-CNN (macro-F1 0.80), which was dropped as too slow to serve.
