## Q1
feat-rs-region-rate was a target-encoded recovery rate by postcode region. It was computed out-of-fold on training data only and smoothed toward the book mean. In exp-303 it lifted rs-xgb-v1's Gini from 0.41 to 0.44 (KS 0.32) on recovery-scoring-holdout-2023h2 (n=6,120). However, exp-304 showed a 2.9x spread in predicted recovery across region deciles at equal balance and payment history, which is the pattern Omar Haddad (compliance) flagged as a proxy for neighbourhood demographics. On 2025-05-22 Lena Fischer and Omar Haddad dropped it from all recovery-scoring models, accepting the 0.02 Gini cost (0.44 to 0.42): it was removed from the rs-xgb-v2 build and excluded from rs-xgb-v3, and rs-xgb-v2 still reached Gini 0.47 without it.

## Q2
exp-403 showed that the naive call effect (open rate 4.9% with a call in the prior 90 days against 3.1% without, +1.8 points) shrinks to +0.6 points (CI -0.1 to +1.3, not significant) after matching on propensity score and tenure. So the gap is mostly who calls, not what the call does. The resulting finding (2025-09-24) was that call-centre outreach is not a program at scale on that basis, which led to the causal forest xs-call-uplift-v1. That model (with exp-406) found +1.2 points (CI 0.4–2.0) in deciles 1–2 of xs-gbm-v2 and about +0.1 (not significant) in the top deciles. Decision on 2025-11-10: call-centre savings outreach goes only to deciles 1–2, and calls to the top deciles stop for this offer.

It takes sfc-lgbm-v2's six per-segment LightGBM base forecasts and reconciles them with the total using MinT, adding the active-account count as a regressor. The base forecasts use feat-sfc-lags-v1 (lags 1/3/12), feat-sfc-season-v2 (Fourier terms plus retail-calendar-v3 events) and feat-sfc-cpi-lag6. Those come from ds-sfc-segment-monthly (cards.accounts_monthly + crm.contacts), the holiday calendar and the CPI macro series; feat-sfc-util-v1 (utilisation) applies only to the Rewards segment, not the other five. Its evidence base is exp-207 / fnd-sfc-q4-reconciled on sfc-oot-2025h2 (total MAPE 2.9%→2.3%, segment 5.1%→4.9%, Q4 bias -3.1%→-0.4%); it is refit monthly and is in turn the spend-volume input for revenue-forecast-2026.
