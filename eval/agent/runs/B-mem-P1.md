## Q1
feat-rs-region-rate is a target-encoded recovery rate by postcode region. It is computed out-of-fold on training data only, smoothed toward the book mean, and sourced from `cards.accounts_monthly` postcode. Adding it lifted Gini from 0.41 to 0.44 on recovery-scoring-holdout-2023h2 (exp-303, n=6,120), and removing it cost 0.02 (0.44 to 0.42, exp-304). exp-304 also showed a 2.9x recovery spread across region deciles, so compliance (Omar Haddad) judged it a proxy for neighbourhood demographics that cannot be used in an agency placement model. On 2025-05-22 Lena Fischer and Omar Haddad decided to drop it from all recovery-scoring models (removed from rs-xgb-v2, excluded from rs-xgb-v3), accepting the 0.02 Gini cost; rs-xgb-v2 later reached Gini 0.47 without it (exp-306).

## Q2
The naive comparison made a call in the prior 90 days look worth +1.8 points of open rate (4.9% with a call vs 3.1% without). Matching on propensity score and tenure cut that to +0.6 points (CI -0.1 to +1.3, not significant), so most of the gap was selection. find-xs-call-overstated concluded that call-centre outreach is not a program at scale. That led to xs-call-uplift-v1, a causal forest that found +1.2 points in the two lowest deciles, and to the decision (dec-xs-call-low-deciles, 2025-11-10) to make savings outreach calls only to deciles 1-2.
Sources: memory/cross-sell-savings/exp-403-callcentre-naive.md, memory/cross-sell-savings/find-xs-call-overstated.md, memory/cross-sell-savings/xs-call-uplift-v1.md, memory/cross-sell-savings/dec-xs-call-low-deciles.md
## Q3
sfc-hier-v3 is built on sfc-lgbm-v2's per-segment LightGBM base forecasts for six segments. It adds MinT reconciliation across segments and the total (exp-207, which took total MAPE from 2.9% to 2.3% on sfc-oot-2025h2), feat-sfc-active-accounts as a regressor, and the reconciled Q4-bias finding (fnd-sfc-q4-reconciled, bias -0.4%). It also uses feat-sfc-cpi-lag6. Downstream, revenue-forecast-2026 depends on it as the spend volume input for interchange (dec-sfc-ship-hier-v3, adopted for FY26).

## Q6
app-scorecard-v3's Gini fell from 0.41 to 0.33 on the 2026-Q1 holdout (n=18,900, bad rate 4.7%), mostly because the v8 bureau score drifted. That raised the question of whether a rebuild could reach OOT Gini 0.42 without manual overrides. Lena Fischer approved the v4 rebuild on 2026-06-26: it keeps the points-based form and adds v10 bureau bands, reject-inference weights from app-gbm-v2, and the income estimate (after exp-506 showed the thin-file lift). The v4 candidate then scored Gini 0.42 against 0.35 for v3 on application-scorecard-oot-2026h1 (exp-507, n=61,700), which led to the new cutoff of 592.

## Q7
feat-txc-normalise-v2 strips store numbers, '#' codes, trailing city and state suffixes and repeated whitespace from descriptors before tokenising. It was built from ds-txc-descriptor-sample (2.1M descriptors, Jan-Jun 2025). It lifted txc-gbm-v2 macro-F1 from 0.78 to 0.81 on holdout-2025q2 (exp-104, n=2,000), mostly in Travel and Fuel. Its rule list is versioned in the repo and new suffixes are added by pull request, not by the model. It feeds feat-txc-descriptor-tokens-v2 and the current model txc-gbm-v3 (macro-F1 0.83 on txc-oot-2026h1, shipped 2026-03-10).
Sources: memory/txn-categorization/feat-txc-normalise-v2.md, memory/txn-categorization/exp-104.md, memory/txn-categorization/feat-txc-descriptor-tokens-v2.md, memory/txn-categorization/txc-gbm-v3.md, memory/txn-categorization/dec-txc-ship-gbm-v3.md
## Q8
Adding the payment-to-minimum ratio (feat-ed-minpay-v2) to ed-gbm-v1, without the limit-change feature, lifted AUC from 0.781 to 0.803 on early-delinquency-holdout-2024q4 (n=402,000), with Gini 0.606 and KS 0.44. find-ed-minpay confirmed it as the real second signal: +0.022 AUC with no leakage. exp-205, together with exp-206, was the basis for ed-gbm-v2 (2025-09-10), which ed-gbm-v3 later superseded (AUC 0.809 on early-delinquency-oot-2026h1).
Sources: memory/early-delinquency/exp-205.md, memory/early-delinquency/find-ed-minpay.md, memory/early-delinquency/ed-gbm-v2.md, memory/early-delinquency/ed-gbm-v3.md
## Q9
fsm-churn-xgb-v2 reads 41 features from the feature store: v1's 40 features, with payment lag v2 (UTC timestamps) and util ratio taken from the store, plus the store-only feat-fsm-spend-cv-30d. It keeps v1's trees and hyperparameters. It scored holdout-2025q2 AUC 0.791 against 0.782 for v1, with KS 0.43 (exp-105, n=412,000). It went into production at the 2026-03-02 cutover behind the parity gate (211 of 214 features passed; util ratio was accepted under its documented rule).

## Q10
The cutoff is 652 on the v10 scale, effective 2026-09-01. It replaces the v8 cutoff of 640 and holds the same 58% approval rate (58.0% vs 58.1%) and 4.1% 60-day bad rate (exp-305, n=71,300). The cutover uses bsm-app-gbm-v10b and bsm-cutoff-logit; Aisha Khan owns the cutover and Sam Lee the model. A v8 fallback stays in place through 31 Dec 2026, because 8% of pulls have no v8 row after the feed change.
