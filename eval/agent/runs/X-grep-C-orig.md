## Q1
feat-velocity-v2 has 6 `vel_` columns: the count and sum of card transactions in the 30 days "around" the observation date, plus their ratio to the 90-day average. It is built on `cards.txn_daily` (`features/velocity_v2.py`). It gave exp-014 its lift (AUC 0.71→0.81, mostly from `vel_ratio_30_90`) and fed churn-xgb-v2, but its window took in days after the observation date, inside the label window, which leaked the target. With those days removed, AUC falls to 0.74. It was retired on 2026-10-30 and replaced by feat-velocity-v3, whose windows use pre-observation days only. Any v2 numbers computed before 2026-10-27 also used about 6% fewer September transactions.
Sources: churn-2026/feat-velocity-v2.md, churn-2026/exp-014.md, churn-2026/post-period-leak.md, churn-2026/dec-velocity-v3.md, churn-2026/feat-velocity-v3.md, churn-2026/txn-backfill.md
## Q2
exp-014 (XGBoost, 400 trees, depth 6, baseline features plus velocity v2) scored AUC 0.81 and recall@10 0.52 on holdout-2025q4 (n=1.2M, 2026-09-25), up from the baseline's 0.71 / 0.29. It became churn-xgb-v2. A 0.10 AUC jump looked too good, and the leak check exp-017 confirmed a leak: with post-period days removed, AUC was 0.74 and recall@10 0.41, and `vel_ratio_30_90` fell from 1st to 9th in gain importance. Its result is overturned by churn-xgb-v3 (AUC 0.76, recall@10 0.43). The notes say "v2's 0.52 was the leak; 0.43 is the honest number."

## Q4
The production model is churn-xgb-v3 (`models:/churn-xgb/3`), which replaced v2 in the nightly scoring job on 2026-10-30. It is XGBoost with v2's settings, on feat-velocity-v3 plus feat-utilization-v1, trained on `cards.churn_labels` for observation months 2025-01 to 2025-09. On holdout-2025q4 (n=1.2M) it scores AUC 0.76 and recall@10 0.43, with no leakage. Recall@10 holds steady across monthly cohorts (Oct 0.45, Nov 0.43, Dec 0.40); for comparison the logistic baseline scored 0.71 / 0.29. It will be retrained monthly on the latest 9 observation months.

## Q5
The 0.81 was inflated by target leakage. feat-velocity-v2 aggregated `cards.txn_daily` over a window "around" the observation date, so it included post-period days inside the 90-day label window. Removing those days alone dropped AUC to 0.74 and recall@10 to 0.41 (exp-017), and `vel_ratio_30_90` fell from 1st to 9th in importance. So v3 rebuilt velocity from pre-observation days only, keeping utilization (checked clean), and scored AUC 0.76 / recall@10 0.43. One minor confound: v2 was evaluated before the 2026-10-27 September backfill, so it used about 6% fewer September transactions.
Sources: churn-2026/leak-hypothesis.md, churn-2026/exp-017-leak-check.md, churn-2026/post-period-leak.md, churn-2026/dec-velocity-v3.md, churn-2026/churn-xgb-v3.md, churn-2026/feat-utilization-check.md, churn-2026/txn-backfill.md

## Q7
The cuts are at churn score 0.50 and 0.20, decided 2026-10-08 with Maya Ortiz against a budget of 4% calls and 12% emails. A score of 0.50 or more gets a call (3.8% of the book), 0.20–0.50 gets an email (11.6%), and below 0.20 gets no action. They were set on churn-xgb-v2 scores, and their validity for v3 is an open question (q-recalibrate-tiers, 2026-10-30). v3's scores are less extreme, so at the same cuts the call tier would shrink from 3.8% to about 2.9% of the book. For now the 0.20 / 0.50 cuts stay in place until that question is answered.

## Q8
The model does poorly on new accounts. On holdout-2025q4, accounts under 6 months' tenure (n=168k, 14% of the book) got AUC 0.69 and recall@10 0.31 from churn-xgb-v2, against AUC 0.83 for tenure of 6 months or more (n=1.03M). New accounts are 21% of churners but have too little history for velocity or utilization to mean much. The options are a tenure-specific model, or routing new accounts to the onboarding team (owner Priya Nair). This was measured on the leaky v2; the notes have no tenure split for v3.
Sources: churn-2026/exp-015-tenure.md, churn-2026/finding-new-accounts.md

## Q10
These are trained on `cards.churn_labels`: logreg-baseline (exp-011), the exp-014 XGBoost, churn-xgb-v2 (`models:/churn-xgb/2`) and the current churn-xgb-v3 (`models:/churn-xgb/3`).
