## Q1
feat-rs-region-rate is the target-encoded (out-of-fold, smoothed toward the book mean) recovery rate by postcode region. Adding it lifted Gini from 0.41 to 0.44 (exp-303), and removing it cost 0.02 Gini (0.44 to 0.42, exp-304). Compliance (Omar Haddad) flagged it as a proxy for neighbourhood demographics, so on 2025-05-22 Lena Fischer and Omar Haddad decided to drop it from all recovery-scoring models. It was removed from the rs-xgb-v2 build and excluded from rs-xgb-v3, and the 0.02 Gini cost was accepted.
Sources: memory/recovery-scoring/feat-rs-region-rate.md, memory/recovery-scoring/exp-303.md, memory/recovery-scoring/exp-304.md, memory/recovery-scoring/find-rs-region-proxy.md, memory/recovery-scoring/dec-rs-drop-region.md
## Q2
In the naive comparison, a call in the prior 90 days looked like +1.8 points of open rate (4.9% vs 3.1%). After matching on propensity score and tenure, the effect was only +0.6 points (CI -0.1 to +1.3, not significant), so most of the gap was selection. The finding concluded call-centre outreach was not worth running at scale. The causal forest xs-call-uplift-v1 then found +1.2 points in deciles 1-2, which led to the decision to limit call outreach to those two deciles.
Sources: memory/cross-sell-savings/exp-403-callcentre-naive.md, memory/cross-sell-savings/find-xs-call-overstated.md, memory/cross-sell-savings/xs-call-uplift-v1.md, memory/cross-sell-savings/dec-xs-call-low-deciles.md
## Q3
sfc-hier-v3 builds on and replaces sfc-lgbm-v2. It uses LightGBM base forecasts for six segments, reconciled with MinT across the segments and the total. It depends on feat-sfc-active-accounts (a new regressor), feat-sfc-cpi-lag6 and feat-sfc-lags-v1. Its basis is exp-207 and fnd-sfc-q4-reconciled: MinT took total MAPE from 2.9% to 2.3% on sfc-oot-2025h2 and Q4 bias to -0.4%. Downstream, revenue-forecast-2026 uses it as the spend-volume input for interchange.

## Q4
The current threshold is 0.66, set when fraud26-gbm-v3 was promoted on 2026-08-26 with no travel-specific uplift. It replaced the earlier 0.62 threshold used with v2. In exp-208, 0.66 gave a 0.6% alert rate, 21.5% precision and 0.61 recall. A later geo-mismatch drift finding (PSI 0.14) did not change the threshold; it only calls for monitoring and a Q4 retrain if PSI stays above 0.10.

## Q6
app-scorecard-v3's Gini fell from 0.41 to 0.33 on the 2026-Q1 holdout (n=18,900), mostly because the v8 bureau score drifted. That raised the question of whether a rebuilt scorecard could reach OOT Gini 0.42 without manual overrides. Lena Fischer approved the v4 rebuild on 2026-06-26. v4 keeps the points-based form and adds v10 bureau bands, reject-inference weights from app-gbm-v2, and the income estimate (added once exp-506 showed the thin-file lift). In exp-507, v4 reached Gini 0.42 against 0.35 for v3, and its approval cutoff is 592 from 2026-11-01.

## Q7
feat-txc-normalise-v2 cleans the descriptor before tokenising: it strips store numbers, '#' codes, trailing city and state suffixes, and repeated whitespace. Its rule list is versioned in the repo, and new suffixes are added by pull request. It lifted txc-gbm-v2 macro-F1 from 0.78 to 0.81 on holdout-2025q2 (exp-104), mostly in 'Travel' and 'Fuel'. It is an input to feat-txc-descriptor-tokens-v2 and to the current shipped model txc-gbm-v3 (macro-F1 0.83 on txc-oot-2026h1).
Sources: memory/txn-categorization/feat-txc-normalise-v2.md, memory/txn-categorization/exp-104.md, memory/txn-categorization/feat-txc-descriptor-tokens-v2.md, memory/txn-categorization/txc-gbm-v3.md, memory/txn-categorization/dec-txc-ship-gbm-v3.md
## Q8
exp-205 added the payment-to-minimum ratio (feat-ed-minpay-v2) to ed-gbm-v1 without the limit-change feature. AUC rose from 0.781 to 0.803 (Gini 0.606, KS 0.44) on early-delinquency-holdout-2024q4 (n=402,000), and the finding confirmed there was no leakage. It became the basis for ed-gbm-v2, which moved onto the watchlist for full-file accounts. ed-gbm-v2 has since been superseded by ed-gbm-v3 (AUC 0.809).
Sources: memory/early-delinquency/exp-205.md, memory/early-delinquency/find-ed-minpay.md, memory/early-delinquency/ed-gbm-v2.md, memory/early-delinquency/dec-ed-gbm-for-watchlist.md, memory/early-delinquency/ed-gbm-v3.md
## Q9
fsm-churn-xgb-v2 reads 41 features from the feature store. These are v1's 40 features, with payment lag v2 (UTC timestamps) and util ratio taken from the store, plus the new feat-fsm-spend-cv-30d. It uses the same trees and hyperparameters as v1 and reached holdout AUC 0.791 against 0.782 for v1 (exp-105). It has been in production since the 2026-03-02 cutover, behind a parity gate of at least 99% of rows within 0.5%.
