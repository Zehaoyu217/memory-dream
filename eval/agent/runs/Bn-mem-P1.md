## Q1
feat-rs-region-rate is an out-of-fold, smoothed target-encoded recovery rate by postcode region, computed on training data only. In exp-303 it lifted Gini from 0.41 to 0.44 (KS 0.32) on recovery-scoring-holdout-2023h2 (n=6,120). Compliance (Omar Haddad) flagged it as a proxy for neighbourhood demographics: exp-304 found a 2.9x recovery spread across region deciles at equal balance and payment history. On 2025-05-22 Lena Fischer and Omar Haddad decided to drop it from all recovery-scoring models (removed from rs-xgb-v2, excluded from rs-xgb-v3) and accepted the 0.02 Gini cost (0.44 to 0.42).

## Q2
exp-403 found a naive +1.8-point open-rate gap for customers who called in the prior 90 days (4.9% vs 3.1%). After matching on propensity score and tenure the effect was only +0.6 points (CI -0.1 to +1.3, not significant), so most of the gap was selection: who calls, not what the call does. The finding (find-xs-call-overstated) was that call-centre outreach is not a program at scale. That led to xs-call-uplift-v1, a causal forest that finds +1.2 points in the two lowest deciles (exp-406), and to the decision to do call-centre savings outreach only for deciles 1-2.
Sources: memory/cross-sell-savings/exp-403-callcentre-naive.md, memory/cross-sell-savings/find-xs-call-overstated.md, memory/cross-sell-savings/xs-call-uplift-v1.md, memory/cross-sell-savings/exp-406-call-uplift.md, memory/cross-sell-savings/dec-xs-call-low-deciles.md
## Q3
sfc-hier-v3 builds on sfc-lgbm-v2 (which it supersedes): it takes LightGBM base forecasts for the six segments and reconciles them with MinT across segments and the total. It adds feat-sfc-active-accounts as a regressor and still uses feat-sfc-cpi-lag6. It rests on exp-207 (MinT takes total MAPE from 2.9% to 2.3% on sfc-oot-2025h2) and fnd-sfc-q4-reconciled (Q4 bias -0.4%). Downstream, revenue-forecast-2026 uses it as the spend volume input for interchange.

## Q6
app-scorecard-v3's Gini fell from 0.41 to 0.33 on the 2026-Q1 holdout (n=18,900), mostly because the v8 bureau score drifted. That prompted Lena Fischer's target of 0.42 out-of-time Gini with no manual overrides, and her approval of the rebuild on 2026-06-26. v4 keeps the points-based form and adds v10 bureau bands (feat-app-bureau-v10-band), reject-inference weights from app-gbm-v2 (worth +0.02 KS) and the income estimate (exp-506: Gini +0.01, thin-file approval up from 19% to 23%). It reached Gini 0.42 against 0.35 for v3 in exp-507 (n=61,700), and its cutoff of 592 takes effect on 2026-11-01.

## Q7
feat-txc-normalise-v2 cleans descriptors before tokenising: it strips store numbers, '#' hash codes, trailing city and state suffixes, and repeated whitespace. Its rule list is versioned in the repo, and new suffixes are added by pull request, not by the model. It lifted txc-gbm-v2 macro-F1 from 0.78 to 0.81 on holdout-2025q2 (exp-104), mostly in Travel and Fuel. It feeds feat-txc-descriptor-tokens-v2 and txc-charcnn-v1, and it is an input to the current txc-gbm-v3 (macro-F1 0.83 on txc-oot-2026h1), shipped to the nightly insights run.
Sources: memory/txn-categorization/feat-txc-normalise-v2.md, memory/txn-categorization/exp-104.md, memory/txn-categorization/feat-txc-descriptor-tokens-v2.md, memory/txn-categorization/txc-gbm-v3.md, memory/txn-categorization/dec-txc-ship-gbm-v3.md
## Q8
exp-205 added the payment-to-minimum ratio (feat-ed-minpay-v2) to ed-gbm-v1 without the limit-change feature. AUC rose from 0.781 to 0.803 on early-delinquency-holdout-2024q4 (n=402,000; Gini 0.606, KS 0.44). The finding was that min-pay is the real second signal (+0.022 AUC) with no leakage, and it should replace the limit-change feature. That produced ed-gbm-v2, later superseded by ed-gbm-v3 (AUC 0.809 on oot-2026h1), which still uses min-pay.
Sources: memory/early-delinquency/exp-205.md, memory/early-delinquency/find-ed-minpay.md, memory/early-delinquency/ed-gbm-v2.md, memory/early-delinquency/ed-gbm-v3.md
## Q9
