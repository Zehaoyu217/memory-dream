## Q1
feat-rs-region-rate was a target-encoded recovery rate by postcode region. It was computed out-of-fold on training data only, smoothed toward the book mean, and built from `cards.accounts_monthly` postcode joined to ds-rs-recovery-labels. Adding it lifted Gini from 0.41 to 0.44 on recovery-scoring-holdout-2023h2 (n=6,120, exp-303). But exp-304 showed that removing it cost only 0.02 Gini (0.44 to 0.42), and that with it kept, predicted recovery differed 2.9x between the top and bottom region deciles at equal balance and payment history; this was the proxy-for-demographics pattern compliance (Omar Haddad) had flagged. On 2025-05-22 Lena Fischer and Omar Haddad decided to drop it from all recovery-scoring models (removed from the rs-xgb-v2 build, excluded from rs-xgb-v3, 0.02 Gini cost accepted), and rs-xgb-v2 later reached Gini 0.47 without it (exp-306).

## Q2
exp-403-callcentre-naive (2025-09-22) found a raw open rate of 4.9% for customers with a call in the prior 90 days against 3.1% without (+1.8 points). Matching on propensity score and tenure cut the gap to +0.6 points (CI -0.1 to +1.3, not significant), so find-xs-call-overstated concluded the gap was mostly selection and that call-centre outreach does not justify a program at scale. That led to the causal-forest study xs-call-uplift-v1 and exp-406, which found a call adds about 1.2 points of open rate, but only in deciles 1-2 of xs-gbm-v2. The decision dec-xs-call-low-deciles (2025-11-10) limits call-centre savings outreach to deciles 1-2 and stops calls to the top deciles for this offer.

## Q6
The deployed app-scorecard-v3 had degraded: its Gini fell from 0.41 to 0.33 on holdout-2026q1 (Jan-Mar 2026 applications, n=18,900, bad rate 4.7%). Most of the fall came from drift in the v8 bureau score, while the utilisation and velocity features stayed stable (find-app-gini-v3-drift). That raised the question of whether a rebuilt scorecard could reach out-of-time Gini 0.42 without manual overrides.
