# Model risk policy, 2026 edition
The Model Risk Management Policy implements supervisory letter SR 11-7.
The Model Risk Committee issued it on 2026-01-15.
Every Tier 1 model is validated before use and reviewed every year.
The policy governs fraud26-gbm-v3, the live card-not-present fraud model.
fraud26-gbm-v3 is a Tier 1 model because it declines payments without a person in the loop.
The Validation Standard is part of the Model Risk Management Policy.
Exceptions are approved by the Model Risk Committee and recorded in the model inventory.
